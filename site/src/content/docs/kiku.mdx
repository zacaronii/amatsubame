Kiku plays the podcasts and audiobooks on your own
[Audiobookshelf](https://www.audiobookshelf.org) server. With [Kaku](/kaku/) running, each episode
comes with a transcript that follows along line by line: tap any word to look it up and send it to
Anki with the line's audio. When your hands are busy, mark the moment with your AirPods and mine it
later.

## What you need

- An Audiobookshelf server, version 2.26.0 or later.
- [Kaku](/kaku/) for transcripts. Without it, episodes still play, just without their text.
- Optional, for mining: Anki with AnkiConnect. See [Lookup and Anki](/lookup-and-anki/).

## Connecting Audiobookshelf

The first time you open Kiku, setup asks for your server address (for example
`https://audiobookshelf.example.com`) and lets you sign in with your **Password** or an **API Key**
from Audiobookshelf. Then it shows your libraries and asks which ones are Japanese. Setup also offers
Kaku as a recommended extra. [Getting started](/getting-started/) covers the rest.

Later, the server is in **Settings → Audiobookshelf**, Kaku in **Settings → Transcripts**, and the
libraries in **Settings → Libraries**. Turn off a library you don't listen to in Japanese.

## Listening

Kiku has four tabs: **Listen**, **Marks**, **Stats** and **Settings**.

- **Listen** starts with **Today**: your listening time today and your streak (tap it for Stats).
  Below it are **Continue Listening**, your **Downloaded** episodes, **New Episodes** from
  your shows (with podcast libraries) and **Recently Added** books (with audiobook libraries),
  with a link to the whole **Library** (filter it by **All**, **Podcasts**,
  **Audiobooks** or **Downloaded**). Search finds shows, books and episodes.
- A show's page lists its episodes (**All**, **Unplayed** or **Downloaded**), with **Play Latest**
  or **Resume**.
- A book's page shows the author and narrator, its chapters, and **Play** or **Resume**. Kiku plays
  audiobooks that are a single file; a book split into several files says so on its page.
- On the Mac, each library is in the sidebar, and its filter is in the toolbar: **All**,
  **Listening**, **Not Started** or **Finished** for books, and **All**, **New Episodes**, **Not
  Started** or **Up to Date** for podcasts, with a **Sort** menu (**Title**, **Recently Added**,
  **Recently Listened**). Downloads are under **On This Mac ▸ Downloaded**.
- On the Mac, **Pin** in a show or book's toolbar adds it to the sidebar's **Pinned** section.
  Right-click a pinned one and choose **Unpin** to take it out.
- If Audiobookshelf can't be reached, the library from the last time it loaded still shows, and
  downloaded episodes play.

### The player

The player shows the transcript, with the line being spoken highlighted and kept in view.

- The buttons either side of play jump to the previous or next **line** (or skip by seconds when
  there's no transcript). The speed button is beside them.
- Tap a line's time to play from there. Scrolling the transcript yourself stops following;
  **Back to Now** picks it up again.
- The speech-bubble button hides the transcript and shows the artwork, with the current line small
  underneath; tap that line to bring the transcript back. Kiku remembers your choice.
- The **⋯** menu has the **Sleep Timer** (15 to 60 minutes, or **End of Episode**), **Share**,
  **Stop Playing**, and for books **Chapters**, **Previous Chapter** and **Next Chapter**.

Kiku shows on the Lock Screen and in Control Center. Their skip buttons (and your AirPods' or car's)
jump by the intervals you set in **Settings → Playback**: 10 to 60 seconds back and forward.

### CarPlay

In the car, Kiku has two tabs: **Listen Now**, with what you're partway through and new episodes,
and **Library**, with your libraries and **Downloaded** (which plays without a signal). Now Playing
has the speed button, **Mark** (the bookmark fills for a moment and a chime plays, so you don't have
to look) and, for books, **Chapters**. There's no transcript in the car. The steering-wheel
previous and next buttons skip back and forward by your skip intervals.

### Apple Watch

Kiku on Apple Watch is a remote for Kiku on your iPhone: it shows what's playing, with skip, play
and pause, and a big **Mark** button. The Digital Crown turns your iPhone's volume. If your iPhone is
out of reach, a mark waits and is saved at the right moment when it's back. Add the Kiku complication
to a watch face to see how far along you are and open the remote with one tap.

**Live Text** puts the transcript on your wrist. Turn it on in **Settings ▸ Playback ▸ Live Text on
Apple Watch** (it's off until you do), then tap the transcript button at the top left of the remote.
The line playing is large, with the lines before it dimmed above.

- **Mark Line** marks the line on screen, so Marks shows exactly that line.
- **Tap a word** to look it up: playback pauses until you close it, your iPhone does the lookup, and the watch shows the reading, the first
  meaning, how common it is and how often you've looked it up. **Add card** mines it on your iPhone
  with the line's audio, just as in the player (it waits in the queue if Anki isn't reachable).
- **Turn the Digital Crown** to scroll back. Tap an earlier line to get **Mark** and **Replay** (which
  plays that line on your iPhone, then carries on), and **Back to now** to follow along again.

Beside **Mark** are a chapters button (for anything with chapters: Previous, Next and the list)
and the speed. Both work for whichever is playing, your iPhone or the watch. In Watch mode the
complication follows the watch's own playback, and Now Playing shows the cover.

Live Text works for episodes playing on the watch too: each one you send takes its transcript
along (the watch fetches it while your iPhone is near), so the lines, Mark Line and Replay work
with your iPhone at home. Lookups still happen on your iPhone: out of its reach, tapping a word
does nothing (use **Mark Line** to look it up in **Marks** later).

#### Listening on the watch

Send an episode or a book to your watch and leave your iPhone at home:

1. On your iPhone, long-press an episode (or use the watch button on a book's page, or **Send to
   Watch** in the player's ⋯ menu). The watch downloads it from Audiobookshelf by itself, most
   readily on its charger. **Settings ▸ Storage ▸ Downloads ▸ On Apple Watch** shows how far along
   each one is and how much space the watch has left. The download starts as soon as the watch
   hears about it if Kiku's complication is on your watch face; otherwise it starts the next time
   you open Kiku on the watch. If your watch can't reach your server (it's only on a VPN or at
   home), download the episode on your iPhone first, then long-press it there and choose **Send from
   iPhone**: your iPhone sends its own copy instead.
2. On the watch, the button at the top right shows where Kiku is playing: an iPhone or a watch.
   Tap it for **Play On** and **On This Watch**, then tap an episode to play it on the watch. Connect
   Bluetooth headphones first (the watch asks if none are connected).
3. Your listening, place and marks go back to your iPhone when it's near, and from there to
   Audiobookshelf and Stats. **Sync Now** on either side sends them straight away, and each shows
   when it last synced. If your iPhone was further along than where the watch got to, Kiku asks
   which place to continue from rather than jumping back.
4. To fill the watch by itself, open **Settings ▸ Downloads ▸ Apple Watch**: **Sync Once Started**
   sends what you start playing on your iPhone, **Download Ahead** keeps the first episodes of each
   show you download ahead, and **Remove Played** clears what you've finished. A storage limit
   keeps the rules in check. Remove something a rule added and it stays off.

Your Audiobookshelf server needs to be reachable from the watch's own Wi-Fi or cellular connection
for it to download. If you're an admin, Kiku adds an API key called "Kiku on Apple Watch" for this,
and removes it when you sign out.

### Transcripts from Kaku

When you open an episode, Kiku asks Kaku for its transcript. If Kaku has it, it appears at once. If
Kaku is still working, the player shows how far along it is, or your place in Kaku's queue, and
opening the episode moves it up. Transcripts you've played or downloaded are saved on your device,
so they work offline.

### Looking words up

Tap any word in the transcript. Playback pauses, and a sheet shows the line (with a button to hear
its clip) above the dictionary popup. Close it and playback carries on. A card mined here gets:

- the line as the sentence,
- the line's audio clip (cut on your device for downloaded episodes, or by Kaku while streaming),
- the episode's cover as the picture,
- the show, episode and time.

## Marks

Sometimes you hear something you want to mine but can't stop to look. Mark it, and come back later.
Kiku saves where you were in the episode; nothing is recorded.

Ways to mark:

- The **bookmark** button in the player, or in CarPlay's Now Playing.
- The **Mark** button on Apple Watch, or **Mark Line** in its Live Text.
- **Triple-press your AirPods**. A short chime confirms it, since you're not looking.
- The **Mark What I Just Heard** action in the Shortcuts app. Put it on the **Action Button**, Back
  Tap or a Siri phrase of your own: it marks whatever Kiku is playing without opening the app, and
  answers "Marked 13:21".

(A double press of your AirPods goes to the next line, as in Miru.)

The **Marks** tab lists your marks by episode. Each one plays the 15 seconds before the mark and
shows the line it's likely about, with one line either side. Tap a word to look it up and mine that
line just as in the player. A mined mark shows what you mined for a day and then goes away; **Clear**
removes one you don't need. If an episode's transcript isn't ready yet, its marks say
**Transcribing** and fill in when Kaku finishes.

The search at the top of Marks looks through your marks and the transcripts of every episode you've
played, on your device, so it works offline too.

## Read in Yomu

If [Yomu](/yomu/) has the same book paired for read-along, **Read in Yomu** (on the book's page, and
in the player's **⋯** menu) pauses Kiku, saves your place, and opens the book in Yomu at the sentence
you were listening to. Yomu tells you if that book isn't paired yet.

## Downloads

Download an episode or a book from its page to listen offline. The audio, cover and transcript all
come along. On iPhone and iPad, downloads wait for Wi-Fi unless **Download over Cellular** is on in
**Settings → Downloads**; nothing downloads unless you ask (or turn on download ahead, below). **Settings → Storage** lists what's on the device by
show, and the saved transcripts. Each download says why it's there (**By Hand**, **Ahead** or
**Started**), and the top of the list shows how much space each takes.

### Download ahead

**Settings → Downloads → Automatic Downloads** (a podcast library) keeps upcoming episodes downloaded for each podcast
library, off until you turn it on:

- **Keep Downloaded:** how many episodes of each show (1 to 10).
- **Order:** **Newest** keeps the latest episodes, for news; **In Order** keeps the next ones after
  the episode you're on, oldest first, for shows you hear from the start.

It covers the shows you've played in the last 15 days, in Kiku or any other Audiobookshelf app. A show
you haven't played for longer is paused, and playing it starts it again. A show's page shows its state
under the title, and its **Download Ahead** button (in the toolbar) sets that show apart:
**Library Setting**, **On** (always, with its own count and order if you like, even when you haven't
played it lately) or **Off** (never). Download ahead itself never deletes anything (see Removing played downloads).

### Download once started

Turn on **Download Once Started** for a library (podcasts or books) in **Settings → Downloads**, and
an episode or book you start playing downloads while you listen. Next time it plays from the device,
even offline. Just opening one doesn't count: it has to play for a few seconds.

### Removing played downloads

**Remove Played Downloads** (also per library, off until you turn it on) removes a download a day
after you finish the episode or mark the book finished, never the one playing. With **Keep Downloads
You Made by Hand** on (the default), only automatic downloads go; turn it off and yours go too.

If you delete a download that download ahead or download once started fetched, Kiku remembers and
won't fetch it again on its own. Downloading it yourself undoes that.

## Progress sync

Your place is saved on the device and sent to Audiobookshelf every 15 seconds while playing, and when
you pause, so the Audiobookshelf web app and other players stay in step. Kiku resumes from whichever
is newer, your device or the server. Progress made offline is sent later.

Your listening also goes to Audiobookshelf as listening sessions, so its stats and listening history
(and tools that read them) show what you listened to and for how long. Listening from before this
arrived is sent once, as one session per sitting.

## Stats

The **Stats** tab shows your listening time by week, month or all time (only real playback counts,
not seeking), along with lookups, cards mined and your streak. Your
[kechimochi](https://github.com/Morgawr/kechimochi) log can show there too (**Settings → Stats**).

## Settings

- **Listening:** Audiobookshelf, Libraries, Transcripts (Kaku), Playback (Lock Screen skip
  intervals) and Downloads.
- **Lookup and Mining:** dictionaries, word audio sources and Anki. See
  [Lookup and Anki](/lookup-and-anki/).
- **Stats:** the kechimochi address.
- **Storage**, **Advanced** (sharing the setup with your other apps, things waiting to sync, Run
  Setup Again, Export Logs) and **About**.
