Yomu is a reader for the manga and EPUB books on your own [Kavita](https://www.kavitareader.com)
server. In manga it finds the speech bubbles and reads them on your device, so you can tap a word in a
picture just like a word in a book. In books you tap the text itself. Either way you get the same
dictionary popup as the other apps, and one tap sends the word to Anki.

## What you need

- A Kavita server with your manga and books, and an **auth key** for your Kavita user (in Kavita:
  User Settings → Auth Keys).
- Optional, for read-along: an [Audiobookshelf](https://www.audiobookshelf.org) server with the
  audiobook, and [Kaku](/kaku/) for its transcript.
- Optional, for mining: Anki with AnkiConnect. See [Lookup and Anki](/lookup-and-anki/).

## Connecting Kavita

The first time you open Yomu, setup asks for your server address (for example
`https://kavita.example.com`) and your auth key, then shows your Kavita libraries and asks which ones
are Japanese. Only those show in Yomu. The rest of setup covers dictionaries, Anki and a few optional
extras; [Getting started](/getting-started/) walks through it.

Later you can change the server in **Settings → Kavita** (with **Test Connection** and **Remove
Server**), and pick libraries again in **Settings → Libraries**. A turned-off library is hidden from
the Library tab and Continue Reading.

## The Library

Yomu has four tabs: **Library**, **Dictionary**, **Stats** and **Settings**.

- **Library** opens with **Continue Reading** (series you've started and haven't finished), then a row
  per library with its newest series and **See All**. A library's page has a filter at the top:
  **All**, **Reading** or **Downloaded**. The button at the top right narrows it to **Unread** or
  **Finished** and sorts it (**Title**, **Recently Added**, **Recently Read**).
- A series page shows its **Volumes** (as a grid or a list), **Chapters** and **Specials**, how far
  you've read each one, and a button to start or continue reading. Touch and hold a volume or
  chapter (or right-click it) to **Mark as Read** or **Mark as Unread**; the **⋯** menu marks the
  whole series (it asks first). Marking isn't counted as reading time, in Yomu or in Kavita.
- On iPad and Mac, your libraries are also listed in the sidebar under the tabs.
- On the Mac, a library's filter is in the toolbar: **All**, **Reading**, **Unread** or **Finished**,
  with a **Sort** menu (**Title**, **Recently Added**, **Recently Read**). Downloaded volumes are in
  the sidebar under **On This Mac ▸ Downloaded**.
- On the Mac, **Pin** in a series page's toolbar adds it to the sidebar's **Pinned** section.
  Right-click a pinned series and choose **Unpin** to take it out.
- **Dictionary** is a plain dictionary search. You can type in kana, kanji or romaji.

Both readers' settings have **Tap to Turn Pages** (on, with the sides swapped for the other hand, or
off so that only swipes turn pages) and, on iPhone and iPad, **Lock Rotation**, which keeps the screen
the way it is while the reader is open. The manga reader also has **Double-Tap to Zoom**; with it on, a
single tap waits a moment to see whether a second follows.

While a book or manga is open on iPhone or iPad the screen stays on, so it doesn't dim in the middle of
a hard page. It can sleep again after 15 minutes without a page turn.

### Want to Read

A series page's **⋯** menu has **Add to Want to Read** (and **Remove from Want to Read**). It's
Kavita's own list, so series you add in Kavita's web reader show too, and Yomu's show there. The
Library home shows them on a **Want to Read** shelf, which leaves out libraries you've turned off in
Settings and isn't there when the list is empty. Changes made offline show at once and are sent
when Kavita is reachable.

### Reading lists

Your Kavita reading lists are listed under **Reading Lists** on the Library home, with the ones
others have promoted. A list opens with its items in order; tap one to read it. Items Yomu can't
open (PDFs, image folders, libraries you've turned off) are dimmed; tapping one, or one that
can't be fetched while offline, says why.

To add a chapter (or a volume that's a single chapter), hold it on its series page and choose
**Add to Reading List…**, then pick a list or make a new one with **New Reading List…**. Volumes
with several chapters are added a chapter at a time. In a list you made, **Edit** lets you
drag items into a new order and swipe to take one out; **Delete Reading List** is in its **⋯** menu.
Taking out the last item keeps the list, empty, until Kavita cleans up empty lists (daily, unless
your server is set otherwise). Promoted lists and ones that sync from outside Kavita are read-only.
Changes made offline show at once and are sent when Kavita is reachable.

A volume opened from a reading list is read in the list's order: at its end, Yomu offers the
list's next item, even when it's in another series or is a book after a manga, with the series'
next volume as a smaller second choice. Items Yomu can't open (PDFs, image folders, hidden
libraries) are passed over, and the offer says how many. Offline, an item that isn't downloaded
is still offered, marked "not on this device". This lasts while the reader is open: picking the
series up later from Continue Reading reads the series as usual. A list shows which items you've
read (a check) or started, the same as their series pages.

### Collections

Your Kavita collections are listed under **Collections** at the end of the Library home, with the
ones other people on your server have promoted. A collection opens as a grid of its series; series
in libraries you've turned off in Settings aren't shown, and it says how many there are. Promoted
collections, and ones synced from AniList, MyAnimeList or another service, are read-only in Yomu.

To put a series in a collection, open the series' **⋯** menu and choose **Collections…**: tap a
collection to add the series or take it out, or **New Collection…** to make one with it. Kavita
deletes a collection when its last series is taken out, so Yomu asks first. **Delete Collection**
is in a collection's top bar. Changes made offline show at once and are sent when Kavita is
reachable.

## Reading manga

Open a volume and swipe, or tap the left or right third of the page, to turn pages. A tap in the
middle shows the controls, with the page slider and the **Aa** button for the reader's settings. Pinch to zoom in (up to 4×). On a keyboard, the arrow keys turn pages, and on the Mac the
**Reader** menu has Next Page and Previous Page (Page Down and Page Up).

The **Aa** settings are kept per series. **Use for All Series** makes them the default for the others.

- **Mode:** **Paged** or **Long strip** (one continuous scroll, with a **Page width** setting).
- **Direction:** **Right to left** or **Left to right**.
- **Two-Page Spreads:** **Automatic** (when the screen is wide enough), **On** or **Off**, and
  **Show Cover Alone** so the pages pair up the way the book was printed.
- **Split Wide Pages** (on by default): in portrait, a two-page scan is shown one half at a
  time, in reading order, so it isn't shrunk to fit. It still counts as one page; turned
  sideways, or with Two-Page Spreads on, it's shown whole.
- **Crop Borders** (off by default): trims a page's blank white or black margins, so the art fills
  more of the screen. It's careful: a page that's mostly blank, a page on grey or yellowed paper, or
  one with only thin margins is shown whole, and a page number in the margin keeps that side. Facing
  pages stay level. Cards still get the whole page. Not in the long strip.

When a chapter ends, Yomu offers to read the next one.

### Auto-scroll

In **Long strip**, the arrow button in the controls starts auto-scroll: the strip moves down by
itself, with a bar at the bottom to pause, change the speed (1 to 10) or close it (**⌘↓** starts and
stops it with a keyboard). It waits while the controls or a lookup are showing and while you scroll
with your finger, then carries on a moment later, and stops at the end of the chapter. The screen
stays on while it runs. It stops by itself after 30 minutes without a touch, key or pointer move,
so a reader left open doesn't scroll on through the book and count it as read.

### Bookmarks

The bookmark button in the controls bookmarks the page you're on (tap it again to remove it).
Bookmarks are kept on Kavita, so they show in Kavita's own Bookmarks page and on your other devices;
made offline, they wait and are sent when Kavita is reachable. Kavita keeps a copy of each
bookmarked page image in its bookmarks folder on the server, as it does for bookmarks made in its
own reader. Your Kavita account needs Kavita's **Bookmark** role; without it, Yomu tells you so
when you tap the button.

A series' bookmarks are listed under **Bookmarks & Highlights** at the end of its page. Tap one to
read from that page, or hold it to remove it.

### Text recognition on manga

To look words up in manga, Yomu first needs its text recognition models. Download them once in
**Settings → Manga Text Recognition → Download Models**. They find the speech bubbles and read them
on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Then:

- **Tap text on a page** to look it up.
- **Long-press and drag** to draw a box over any area (a sign, a sound effect, text the detector
  missed) and read just that.
- In the **Aa** settings, **Show text boxes** outlines what was found, and **OCR pages ahead** sets
  how many pages Yomu reads before you reach them, so the popup is instant.

Downloaded volumes can be read ahead of time too: **Settings → Downloads → Read Text on Downloaded
Pages**. Then lookups in them work instantly, even offline.

Yomu finds each character in the lettering itself, so a tap lands on the character under your
finger. While a word is looked up it's marked on the page, right on its characters, so you can see
what the tap was taken for. Lettering it can't place exactly (hand lettering, sound effects, a title drawn at angles) gets no
mark; a tap there still goes to the nearest character. Turn the mark off with **Mark the Word on the Page** in the reader's settings.

## Reading books

EPUB books open in a reader built for Japanese: vertical text, right-to-left pages, and furigana.
Swipe, or tap near the left or right edge, to turn the page. A tap in the middle shows the controls,
with **Contents** for the table of contents and **Aa** for settings.

**Aa** settings are kept per series, and **Use for All Books** makes them the default:

- **Theme:** System, Light, Sepia or Dark.
- **Text:** the font (the book's own, 明朝 or ゴシック, or one you added), size, line spacing and side
  margins. **Added Fonts** takes a font file from Files (TrueType, OpenType or WOFF, up to 50 MB, up to
  20 fonts) to read in; touch and hold one to rename or remove it. Added fonts stay on this device and
  are used only for books.
- **Layout:** **Paged** or **Scrolling**, and the **Text direction**: **As in book**, **Vertical** or
  **Horizontal**.
- **Furigana:** **From book** or **Hidden**.
- **Mark words by Anki state:** underlines words you haven't mined and words you're still learning;
  words you know stay plain.
- **Progress text:** **Always**, **With controls** or **Hidden**, plus a thin **Progress bar** along
  the bottom of the screen. The text shows the percentage and characters read of the whole book, and
  the time left at your reading speed.

Tap a word to look it up. While the controls are showing, the first tap only hides them, and the next
one looks up.

**Search** (the magnifier in the controls, or **⌘F** with a keyboard; while a book is open,
**Search Dictionary** moves to **⇧⌘F**) finds text anywhere in the
book: hiragana and katakana find each other, and case and full or half width don't matter. Results
are listed by chapter with a little text around each; tap one to go there, with the match marked.
The first search in a book reads it through once (**Preparing search…**), and the last search stays
in the field while the book is open. Going to a result is only a look: your reading place doesn't
move until you turn a page from there, and **Back to Where You Were** returns. (Choosing a chapter in
**Contents**, by contrast, moves your reading place at once.)

In the **Scrolling** layout, the arrow button in the controls starts **auto-scroll** at a reading
speed in characters a minute (shown in its bar at the bottom, from 100 to 1,000 字/分), whatever the
font size. It waits while the controls or a lookup are showing and while you scroll yourself, goes on
into the next chapter of the book, and stops at the book's end; the screen stays on while it runs.
Like the manga one, it stops after 30 minutes without a touch, key or pointer move. Paged layouts
don't auto-scroll, and read-along takes over the page when it's on.

### Highlights, notes and bookmarks

Above the lookup popup is a row of five colours. Tap one to highlight the sentence the word is in;
tap another to change its colour. The note button writes a note on it (highlighting the sentence
first if it isn't yet), and the bin removes the highlight. Looking up a word in a highlighted
sentence brings back its colour, note and bin. Notes are plain text in Yomu: a note formatted in
Kavita's web reader keeps its formatting when you change the colour, and becomes plain text if you
change its words here.

The bookmark button in the controls bookmarks the place you're reading, named by the words it
starts with; tap it again there to remove it.

Highlights, notes and bookmarks are kept on Kavita: they show in Kavita's own reader and on your
other devices, and the ones made there show in Yomu. Made offline, they wait and are sent when Kavita
is reachable. Highlights and notes need Kavita 0.8.8 or later; on an older server Yomu doesn't offer
them, and any made before it found out stay on your device, marked as not on Kavita yet, until the
server is updated. Whether other people on your server see your highlights is your own Kavita
setting (**Share Annotations**, off unless you turn it on); Yomu doesn't change it.

A book's highlights and bookmarks are listed under **Bookmarks & Highlights** at the end of its page,
with their notes. Tap one to read from there, or hold it to remove it.

### Character counts

Kavita's word counts don't work for Japanese, so Yomu counts characters itself: letters only (kana,
kanji and so on), without punctuation, spaces or furigana. The counts appear on series tiles, the
series page and Continue Reading, and feed the time-left estimate and Stats.

A book is counted when you open it. To count your whole library ahead of time, go to **Settings →
Storage → Character Counts** and tap **Count Characters**. It covers light novel libraries, and
books Kavita marks as Japanese in other book libraries. Until a volume is counted, Yomu shows an
estimate with a "~" in front.

The line at the bottom of a book shows how far you are, and once Yomu knows your reading speed, about
how long is left in the chapter and in the book.

## Read-along

Read-along plays an audiobook while you read the same book: the sentence being spoken lights up and
the pages turn with it. It needs both servers: Audiobookshelf has the audio, and Kaku has its
transcript. Set them up in **Settings → Read-Along**.

To pair a book with its audiobook:

1. Open the series page of an EPUB series. It has a **Read-Along** section.
2. From its menu, choose **Pair with an Audiobook…**. Audiobooks with a matching title are
   suggested; you can also search by title or author.
3. Choose **Get Ready**. Yomu matches the book's text with Kaku's transcript on your device, which
   takes about a minute. If Kaku hasn't transcribed the audiobook yet, Yomu asks it to start; come
   back and get it ready again when it's done.

The section then shows how many of the book's sentences matched (front matter, contents pages and
afterwords usually aren't narrated). The same menu has **Download Audio** for listening offline
(it shows its progress and can be cancelled, and keeps going if you leave the series page; keep
Yomu open), **Match Again**, **Pair with Another Book…** and **Unpair**, which also deletes the
book's downloaded audio unless another volume reads along with it (it asks first when it would;
so does **Pair with Another Book…** for the old book's audio). **Settings ▸ Storage** shows
how much read-along audio is downloaded and can remove it. If the audio can't be loaded, the
reader's bar says why and has **Try Again**.

In the reader:

- An audio bar with play and pause, previous and next sentence, and speed comes and goes with the
  controls.
- Turning or scrolling pages yourself stops following the audio and shows **Follow Audio** to jump
  back.
- Looking up a word pauses the audio and offers **From Here**, to keep listening from that sentence.
  A card mined while reading along gets the sentence's audio.
- The headphones button in the top bar turns read-along off for that volume (plain reading), and on
  again from the page you're on.
- The screen doesn't dim while the audio plays.

Your place goes to both Kavita and Audiobookshelf, and the listening shows in Audiobookshelf's stats
as sessions from Yomu. If you listened further in [Kiku](/kiku/) since,
the book opens at that sentence. Kiku's **Read in Yomu** opens the paired book straight at the
sentence you were listening to.

## Downloads and offline reading

On a series page, download single volumes or the whole series. Nothing downloads unless you ask, or
unless you turn on automatic downloads:

- **Settings → Downloads → Automatic Downloads** works per library. Once you start a series, the
  volume you're reading and the next few (**Download Ahead**) download by themselves.
  **Remove Finished Volumes** removes volumes from the device once you've read them to the end.
- On iPhone and iPad, downloads wait for Wi-Fi unless **Download over Cellular** is on.

When you're offline, or Kavita can't be reached, the Library shows only the series you've
downloaded. **Settings → Storage** shows what's on the device (downloads, dictionaries, text
recognition models and the cache) and lets you remove downloads or clear the cache.

## Progress sync

Your reading position goes back to Kavita, so other Kavita apps and the web reader pick up where you
stopped. If you read offline, the progress waits and is sent when Kavita is reachable again; the
status card at the top of Settings shows anything still waiting, with **Sync Now**. Bookmarks go
the same way.

## Stats

The **Stats** tab shows your reading by week, month or all time: time spent reading, characters read
in books, pages read in manga, lookups, cards mined and your streak. The chart counts time to begin
with, which covers books and manga alike; tap its title to count characters or pages instead. If you keep an immersion log in
[kechimochi](https://github.com/Morgawr/kechimochi), add its address in **Settings → Stats** and the
Stats tab can show it too. Yomu never sends anything to it.

**Daily Goal** (on the chart) sets a goal of characters, pages or minutes a day. The chart then
draws it as a dashed line and says on how many days you met it, and the Today row on the Library
shows a ring that fills as you read. The first time you reach it in a day, the reader says "Daily
goal reached". A goal you change counts from that day on; earlier days keep the goal they had.
Today's progress includes your other devices when Stats syncs through iCloud, but the goal itself
is kept on this device.

## Settings

- **Reading:** Kavita, Libraries, Downloads, Manga Text Recognition, Read-Along and Kaku.
- **Lookup and Mining:** dictionaries, word audio sources, Anki, and word marks in books (manga
  pages aren't marked). See [Lookup and Anki](/lookup-and-anki/).
- **Stats:** the kechimochi address, and **Sync Stats with iCloud**, which keeps the Stats tab the
  same on all your devices signed in to the same iCloud account (on unless you turn it off).
- **Storage**, **Advanced** (sharing the setup with your other apps, things waiting to sync, Run
  Setup Again, Export Logs) and **About**.
