# Demo content credits

The demo servers that Setup offers (Kavita, Jellyfin and Audiobookshelf) hold only openly licensed or
public-domain Japanese content, so anyone can try the apps without a library of their own. Each item
also carries its credit in its own metadata (the EPUB's colophon page and `dc:rights`, the CBZ's
`ComicInfo.xml`, the audiobook's `desc.txt`, the film's `.nfo`). Licences were checked on each source
on 2026-11-27.

## Manga (Kavita → Yomu)

*Pepper&Carrot*, episodes 1–12 in Japanese: art and story by David Revoy,
[www.peppercarrot.com](https://www.peppercarrot.com), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Japanese translation: guruguru. The pages are unmodified, zipped as CBZ. Since 2026-10-08 the author
labels the work "CC BY 4.0, AI derivation prohibited"
([davidrevoy.com/article1178](https://www.davidrevoy.com/article1178)): no AI-generated stories, images
or comics from it. Hosting the pages and looking words up in them isn't a derivation.
