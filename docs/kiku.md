# Kiku (the audio app): working notes

Target and scheme `Kiku` (folder `Kiku/`, tests `KikuTests` and `KikuUITests`), `Kiku.app`, bundle `com.amatsubame.kiku`. Read this when working on Kiku. The plan is [AUDIO_PLAN.md](AUDIO_PLAN.md); shared notes are in [shared.md](shared.md); the transcriber is [kaku.md](kaku.md).

- **Tabs:** Listen (Today, Continue Listening, New Episodes, Library, show and book pages), Marks (marks to mine later, and search across your marks and played episodes' transcripts), Stats (the shared `StatsView`, kind `.listening`) and Settings.
- **Servers:**
  - **Audiobookshelf:** `Packages/AudiobookshelfKit` (`AudiobookshelfClient`). Sign in with a password (`/login` with `x-return-tokens: true`; the access token lasts an hour, the refresh token 30 days, both in the Keychain, refreshed once on a 401 by `AudiobookshelfTokens`) or a pasted API key (no refresh).
  - **Kaku:** `KakuClient` in the same package: URL and token in Settings. `POST /transcripts/status` gives the badges for a whole page in one request.
- **Audiobooks** (milestone 4): a book is one long "episode" with an empty id (`AudiobookshelfEpisode.wholeBook`, from the expanded item), keyed by its item id like Kaku keys it, so progress (`PATCH /api/me/progress/{item}`), transcripts, downloads and marks work as for podcasts. `EpisodeEntry.book` carries the item for chapters, the author (its `showTitle`) and the file count.
  - **Library:** `PodcastLibrary` loads book libraries too (`books`; `bookDetails` with chapters and tracks, saved as `book:<id>` for offline). Settings ▸ Libraries Shown in Kiku hides libraries (`ListenSettings.hiddenLibraries`, Core migration `v22_kiku_books`).
  - **Screens:** Audiobooks in the Library filter ("Audiobook · 38%"), books in Continue Listening and search, `BookView` (author · narrator, Resume/Play, download, chapters). The player shows the chapter under the title and has Chapters, Previous and Next Chapter in its more menu.
  - **Limits:** single-file books only for now (a multi-file book's page says so; Kaku already transcribes them on one timeline). MiscInfo is "Book — Chapter · 1:13:19".
- **Playback** (`Kiku/Playback`):
  - `AudioPlayer` (in AppCommon since milestone 5, shared with Yomu's read-along) wraps AVPlayer: the token goes in a header (`AVURLAssetHTTPHeaderFieldsKey`), never the URL. `.playback`/`.spokenAudio` session; Now Playing and remote commands (play/pause, skip back/forward with the Settings intervals, scrubbing). Artwork is built off the main actor: MediaPlayer asks for it on its own queue.
  - **Play the Next Episode** (`ListenSettings.playsNextEpisode`, on by default; Settings ▸ Playback): `PlaybackModel.finished` asks `KikuEnvironment.playNext`, which starts the show's next episode by date that isn't finished (`PodcastLibrary.episode(after:)`), leaving the player open or small as it was. Not for books, and not when the sleep timer was End of Episode. A finished episode stays finished when its place is saved again at the end (`playedToEnd`). **Resuming** after a pause of 2 s or more starts the cut-off line over (`TranscriptFollow.resumeTarget`).
  - **Lines and shows:** More ▸ Lines has Pause After Each Line (`ListenSettings.pausesAfterLines`) and Repeat the Line, both acting in `tick` when `TranscriptFollow.lineJustEnded`, and the transcript's text size (`transcriptScale`). A show or book can keep its own speed and seconds to skip at the start and end (`ShowListenSetting`, table `showListenSetting`; the speed menu's Only for This Show, and the show's settings sheet). Skipping the end finishes the episode there, so Play the Next Episode follows. Keys in the player: ← → lines, R replay, [ ] speed, M mark, T transcript; a mark gives a haptic.
  - **Small conveniences:** an episode row's menu (`EpisodeMenu`) marks it played or unplayed (`KikuEnvironment.setPlayed`: local progress, the library's copy and a progress job in the outbox, like listening; not for the one playing). A held transcript line offers Copy Line (`Clipboard`, AppCommon) and Play from Here. The sleep timer has End of Chapter for books (`SleepTimer.endOfChapter`, checked on each tick). The timer fades the volume out over its last 30 s of listening (`SleepTimerRules.volume`, set on each tick through `AudioPlayer.volume`; chapter and episode ends count at the speed playing, the episode's end being the show's sign-off when one is skipped). Shake to extend (iOS, `ShakeDetector`: the accelerometer, so it works with the screen off while playing; three jolts over 1.3 g within a second; watched only while a timer of minutes runs or for `shakeGrace`, 2 minutes, after one stopped playback) restarts a timer of minutes or plays on with it set again (`SleepTimerRules.shake`); End of Chapter and End of Episode aren't extended. The grace is armed only when a timer of minutes stopped playback itself (`armsResume`: not after a pause by hand), and a play or pause by hand ends it. Known limit, unverified: after the timer has paused playback, iOS can suspend Kiku within seconds in the background or with the screen locked, and then nothing reads the accelerometer, so the two-minute resume may only work with Kiku on screen (no silent audio is played to keep it awake). Find in Transcript (⋯ menu, ⌘F; `TranscriptFind`): the lines holding the text, matched like the Marks search, starting from the line playing, one shown at a time with its match marked; finding stops following like a scroll does.
  - **Up Next** (#180): `UpNextStore` (Core `v67_up_next`: `upNextItem` per podcast library in order, with titles for showing it offline, and `upNextPlaylist`, the playlist's id) and `KikuEnvironment`'s `upNext` (`Listen/UpNext.swift`). `EpisodeMenu`'s Play Next / Play Last (not for books, nor the one playing); Listen's `UpNextShelf` (the first three) and `UpNextView` (reorder, swipe, Clear). An episode leaves it as it starts (`play` → `leaveUpNext`). When one ends, `playNext(after:)` asks `UpNextChoice.after` (Core, tested): the first in its library's Up Next (else any library's), else the show's next when that setting is on, else nothing; one gone from the server is skipped. Kept on Audiobookshelf as the podcast library's playlist named "Up Next": each change queues the whole list (`AudiobookshelfUpNextPayload`, coalesced per library) and `AudiobookshelfUpNextHandler` makes the playlist match. It uses the playlist whose id is kept, else the user's own "Up Next", else makes one. Checked against 2.38.0 on the test account: a reorder must keep the same items (another set is 400), and removing the last item deletes the playlist, so new items go in before old ones come out, then the order is set. A 404 (deleted elsewhere) forgets the id and starts again once; a second 404 fails the job for good. `refreshUpNext` (after the library's refresh) takes the server's list for each podcast library unless a change made here is still waiting. Tests: `AudiobookshelfUpNextTests` (a fake server with those rules), `UpNextStoreTests`, and `testUpNextFromEpisodeMenus` (DevSetup; with `UITEST_ABS_URL/USER/PASSWORD` it also checks the playlist on the server, and Clear leaves none). CarPlay: Now Playing's Up Next button ("Up Next" for an episode, while the queue has anything; "Chapters" for a book as before) lists the queue (`CarPlaySections.upNext`). The player's ⋯ menu has Play Next in Up Next. Next track isn't remapped: the AirPods' double press stays the next line and a car's wheel a skip. Not yet: the watch, download-ahead.
- **Widgets, Control Center and Siri** (#180):
  - **Intents** (`Packages/KikuIntents`, shared with the extension): `ResumeIntent` ("Resume in Kiku") and `MarkIntent` ("Mark in Kiku", moved here from the app). Both are `AudioPlaybackIntent`s, so the system runs them inside Kiku, in the background if needed. They call `KikuControls.shared`, which `KikuApp.init` fills in: `resumeListening()` plays what's loaded, else the first in Continue Listening; `mark(.shortcut)` marks. `KikuShortcuts` offers both to Siri and Shortcuts (`KikuApp: AppIntentsPackage` includes the package). These work without the widget extension.
  - **The snapshot** (`WidgetSnapshot`, `WidgetSnapshotStore` in the App Group's `Widgets/`): what's playing or last played, playing or not, and the next two (Up Next, else Continue Listening), with small cover JPEGs. `KikuWidgetPublisher` (`Kiku/Widgets/`) writes it half a second after playback, the queue or the library change, only when it changed, then reloads the widgets. The widgets never call a server, so no token leaves Kiku. `KikuWidgetPublisher.snapshot` is tested in `WidgetSnapshotBuilderTests`.
  - **The extension** (`KikuWidgets/`, iOS only): the Now Playing widget in small and medium (cover, title, time left, a play button running `ResumeIntent`; medium adds the next two) and Lock Screen circular (progress) and rectangular (title, show, time left), plus Control Center's Resume and Mark (`ControlWidgetButton`). The Mac has no widgets for now.
  - **Build:** the extension is in `kiku-widgets.yml`, always included (it was opt-in behind `KIKU_WIDGETS=YES` until its App ID signed). Without the App Group, Kiku is unchanged and Siri's Resume and Mark still work.
  - **The developer account:** the App ID `$(BUNDLE_ID_PREFIX).kiku.widgets` with the **App Groups** capability (`group.com.amatsubame`). Automatic signing (`-allowProvisioningUpdates`) registers it and makes its profiles at the first upload; the App Group may need ticking on it by hand. App Store Connect needs nothing: the extension ships inside Kiku. Another team (own `BUNDLE_ID_PREFIX`) gets the same at its first device build.
  - **Pull down to close** (iPhone, iPad): the player is a full-screen cover with a clear presentation background and its own backing, so `PlayerView.pullDown` (on the top bar, the artwork and the controls, alongside their buttons; not the transcript) can move it with the finger and show the app behind. It closes past 110 pt or on a flick (`pullCloses`); the pull is `@GestureState`, so a cancelled drag lets go. The backing reaches over the status bar by `topInset`, since a moved view no longer extends into the top safe area. The mini player opens on a swipe up too.
  - `PlaybackModel`: resume from the newer of local and Audiobookshelf progress, the transcript (saved copy, replaced quietly when Kaku's lines differ after a `kaku.resplit` or Kaku made it again; More ▸ Transcript says what made it, from `AudioTranscriptInfo.model`/`kakuVersion`/`transcribedAt`, and **Transcribe Again** asks Kaku 1.6 with `force` and swaps the new one in when it's made; else Kaku: ready, waiting with `POST /jobs` to move it up, or missing), the current line (`TranscriptFollow`), line jumps, follow (追従), the sleep timer.
  - **Progress:** saved locally and queued every 15 s and on pause (`PATCH /api/me/progress/{item}/{episode}` through the outbox, coalesced per episode). A late report is dropped if Audiobookshelf has something newer.
  - **Listening sessions** (#103; Audiobookshelf's stats and history, `GET /api/me/listening-sessions`): `ListeningSessionReporter` (AppCommon) turns the same listening seconds the Stats tab counts into Audiobookshelf local sessions (`AudiobookshelfSessionPayload`, `POST /api/session/local-all` through the outbox, coalesced per session). A session is one item until a pause longer than `ActivityLog.gap` (5 min) or another item. Its totals go about once a minute while playing and on pause or close. The server creates a session the first time it sees its id (made on the device) and afterwards replaces currentTime, timeListening and updatedAt, so a retry never counts twice; it fills mediaMetadata, the titles and the duration itself, and moves progress only when the session is newer. `deviceInfo` is "Kiku" with a per-install id (`AudiobookshelfDeviceInfo.current`). Kiku never calls `/play` (it streams the file directly), so no live session is open on the server. The progress PATCH stays: sessions never mark an item finished. **Backfill:** once per install, `ListeningSessionBackfill` queues one session for each Stats listening row from before the reporter first ran (`audiobookshelf.sessionsReportedSince`; later rows were reported live), with an id made from the device and the row (sending again changes nothing), timed from the row's start to its end, no later than the progress Kiku saved then, so it doesn't move a place (checked live: `progressSynced: false`). Stats rows have no positions or episode titles: positions are the item's last saved place, and the handler looks up a missing episode title (`GET /api/items/{id}`) before sending, since the server would name the session after the show. It runs at the top of `KikuEnvironment.start()`, before `common.start()` (whose first sync sends it): that and the downloader's start keep observing and never return, so nothing may wait on them. The queue is Advanced ▸ Waiting to Sync ▸ Listening Sessions for Audiobookshelf. `AudiobookshelfSessionLiveTests` sends one through the client to a real server when `LIVE_ABS_URL`, `LIVE_ABS_USER` and `LIVE_ABS_PASSWORD` (the test account) are set; delete the session afterwards with an admin key. Checked against Audiobookshelf 2.36.1; deleting a session needs an admin (`DELETE /api/sessions/{id}`).
  - **Listening time:** `ActivityLog` with kind `.listening`, only for real playback (not seeks).
- **Lookup and mining:** tapping a word pauses and opens `KikuLookupSheet` (the line with its clip, then the shared popup); closing resumes unless you pressed play yourself.
  - **Clips:** the line with 0.15 s before and 0.35 s after, AAC `.m4a`. Downloaded episodes and books are cut on the device with `AVAssetExportSession` (offline mining). It can't cut from a stream (`-11838`), so a streamed one asks Kaku's `/clips` first (`EpisodeAudio.remoteClip`, a few KB); when Kaku can't be reached (e.g. it's only on your home network) the whole file is fetched once to a temporary file and deleted when the player closes.
  - **Picture:** the episode cover. MiscInfo: "Show — Episode · 13:19" (`CardSource.miscInfo`).
- **Marks** (milestone 3, a prototype: mark now, mine later):
  - **Making one:** `KikuEnvironment.mark(_:)` saves the episode and the playing position (`AudioMarkStore`, Core migration `v21_kiku_marks`); nothing is recorded or downloaded. Six ways: the player's bookmark button; CarPlay's Mark button (below); Apple Watch's Mark button, and Live Text's Mark Line (source `watchLine`, at the line's start; Marks treats it like the player's button and shows that line rather than guessing the one before) (docs/watch.md); an AirPods triple press (milestone 6: previous track, through `AudioPlayer.onPreviousTrack`; a two-note `MarkChime` plays through the episode's audio session to confirm, since you aren't looking; a double press, next track, goes to the next line as in Miru; in a car both skip instead, below); `MarkIntent` ("Mark in Kiku", an `AudioPlaybackIntent`, so it runs inside Kiku without opening it, for the Action Button, Siri, Back Tap and Shortcuts; it reaches the environment through `AppDependencyManager`). Now Playing's `bookmarkCommand` was tried and removed: iOS 26 shows it neither on the Lock Screen nor in Control Center.
  - **Action Button setup:** make a shortcut with Kiku's "Mark What I Just Heard" action in the Shortcuts app, then pick it under Settings ▸ Action Button ▸ Shortcut (or Back Tap). Its name is also a Siri phrase. It answers "Marked 13:21" or "Nothing is playing in Kiku". The built-in phrase "Mark in Kiku" may not work, probably because Siri doesn't recognise "Kiku"; a shortcut with your own name for it does.
  - **The Marks tab** (`MarksModel`): grouped by episode; each mark plays the 15 s before it and shows the line it's likely about (`TranscriptFollow.markedIndex`: the line that ended just before, unless the mark is 3 s or more into the line playing) with one line either side. Tapping a word looks it up and mines that line as in the player (`EpisodeAudio`, shared with `PlaybackModel`). `MiningContext.mined(_:)` records the word on the mark, and `MiningService.onCancelled` (Undo) clears it. A mined mark shows for a day, then is pruned. Marked episodes without a saved transcript are fetched from Kaku (and requested if missing); until then they show "Transcribing".
  - **Notes on marks** (#180): `AudioMark.note` (Core migration `v62_audio_mark_note`, synced with the mark through Sync with iCloud: docs/shared.md ▸ Synced records; `AudioMarkStore.setNote` trims it, caps it at `AudioMark.noteLimit` = 4000 characters, and a blank one clears it). Written in `MarkNoteSheet` (Return a new line, ⌘Return or Done saves): from a mark card's Note button or its context menu (cards sit in a scroll view, so no swipe), and from the player's mark toast, which offers Add Note and stays 4 s (2 s otherwise); only its button takes taps, and a tap anywhere else on the player puts it away. Shown under the mark (four lines, tap for all); the Marks search matches notes like lines (`MarksModel.noteMatches`). Not on cards mined from the mark, not on the watch or CarPlay, not sent to Audiobookshelf.
- **CarPlay** (iOS only, `Kiku/CarPlay`): a `CPTemplateApplicationSceneDelegate` (`CarPlaySceneDelegate`, in Info.plist's scene manifest; the generated manifest is off for Kiku, since it would replace that entry). It uses the app's one environment through `KikuEnvironment.current` (set in `KikuApp.init`, which runs even when CarPlay launches the app with no phone window) and starts it and the library if the phone hasn't.
  - **Lists:** `CarPlaySections` turns the library into plain rows (tested in `CarPlaySectionsTests`), and `KikuCarPlay` makes them `CPListTemplate`s that rebuild through Observation while shown. Two tabs: Listen Now (Continue with each row's progress, then New Episodes) and Library (the libraries not hidden, then shows or books, then episodes; and Downloaded, which plays with no signal). Lists are cut to `CPListTemplate.maximumItemCount` and `maximumSectionCount`, ending with "N more on your iPhone". Covers come through `CoverLoader` at `CPListItem.maximumImageSize`, after a placeholder. No transcript: it's not for reading while driving.
  - **Now Playing:** `CPNowPlayingTemplate.shared`, filled by `AudioPlayer`'s Now Playing info and remote commands. Buttons: speed (`CPNowPlayingPlaybackRateButton`, stepping through `ListenSettings.speeds` and wrapping) and Mark (`bookmark`: `mark(.carPlay)`, `MarkChime`, and `bookmark.fill` for 2 s, the only confirmation). Books get the Up Next button titled "Chapters", a list that seeks to a chapter's start.
  - **Entitlement:** `com.apple.developer.carplay-audio`, in Kiku only (`Kiku/Resources/Kiku-*.entitlements`). Simulator builds always have it; device builds only with `AMATSUBAME_CARPLAY = YES` in `Config/Signing.local.xcconfig`, which needs the entitlement granted to your team and the CarPlay Audio capability on Kiku's App ID.
  - **Testing:** Simulator ▸ I/O ▸ External Displays ▸ CarPlay, or Apple's CarPlay Simulator (Additional Tools for Xcode) with a device. XCUITest can't drive CarPlay.
  - **Steering-wheel buttons:** they send previous and next track, like AirPods, but while the audio goes to a car (`PlaybackModel.playsInCar`: the route's output is `.carAudio`) they skip back and forward by the skip intervals instead. Checked in a real car: marking from the wheel worked, but skipping was what the owner expected, and CarPlay's Mark button is on screen. AirPods and other Bluetooth headphones keep mark and next line.
- **Apple Watch:** a remote with Mark and a complication, and Live Text (the transcript on the watch, with lookups and mining done on the phone; off until Settings ▸ Playback turns it on, `ListenSettings.watchLiveText`, Core migration `v40_watch_live_text`). The phone does everything. See [docs/watch.md](watch.md).
- **Downloads:** `EpisodeDownloader` (background URLSession, like Miru's): the audio, the cover and the transcript, in `Application Support/Downloads`. Settings ▸ Storage lists them by show.
  - **Download ahead** (#111, docs/KIKU_PLAN.md milestone 1): `AudioAutoDownloadManager` (Kiku/Downloads/AutoDownloads.swift) with Core's `AudioAutoDownloadSetting` (migration `v41_audio_auto_downloads`; scope `library:<id>` or `show:<item id>`: count, `newest`/`inOrder`, and a show's `isOn`: nil follows its library, true always, false never). `AudioAutoDownloadPlanner` (AudiobookshelfKit, unit-tested) decides: a library's rule covers a show last played within 15 days (Stats rows here, or Audiobookshelf progress from any app), otherwise it's paused; Newest keeps the latest N less finished ones, In Order the next N unfinished from the latest started. It only adds downloads (`DownloadedAudio.reason = .ahead`; nil is by hand); removing is #113's rule below. It runs after the library refreshes (launch, coming back, pull to refresh), when the player closes or moves to another episode, when one finishes and when a setting changes; online only, and the downloader keeps waiting for Wi-Fi unless cellular is on. Screens: Settings ▸ Download Rules (per library) and the show page's toolbar button and status line.
  - **Download once started** (#112): a library's `onceStarted` (migration `v42_download_once_started`, podcast and book libraries). `PlaybackModel.flushListening` calls `AudioAutoDownloadManager.startedPlaying` once per episode, on its first logged listening (about 10 s, so opening doesn't count); it downloads with `reason = .started` if nothing is on the device. The playing stream isn't swapped for the file; the next open plays it from the device.
  - **Remove played downloads** (#113): a library's `removesPlayed` and `keepsManual` (default on), migration `v43_remove_played_downloads`. `AudioAutoDownloadPlanner.playedToRemove`: finished at least a day ago (Audiobookshelf's `lastUpdate`), never the playing key, and with `keepsManual` not by hand (`reason == nil`). It runs first in every `run()`, offline too, from the last progress seen. Deleting an automatic download yourself (`EpisodeDownloader.delete`, not `byRule`) records its key in `audioDownloadDeclined`; download ahead and once started skip declined keys, and a download by hand clears it.
  - **Reasons on show** (#114): Settings ▸ Storage ▸ Downloaded puts each row's reason first in its detail (By Hand / Ahead / Started, from `DownloadedAudio.reason`) and, once anything automatic is there, the space per reason under the total.
- **Read in Yomu** (milestone 6, `Kiku/Listen/ReadInYomu.swift`): on every book page and in the player's ⋯ menu for a book. It pauses, saves the place, waits up to 3 s for the outbox to send it, then opens `amatsubame-yomu://read-along?item=<item id>&t=<seconds>`. Kiku can't see which books Yomu has paired (separate databases), so Yomu explains when one isn't. If Yomu isn't installed, an alert says so.
- **Saved transcripts:** `AudioTranscriptStore` (Core, migration `v19_kiku`): sentences with word timings and an FTS5 trigram index (LIKE under three characters), keyed `<item>/<episode>`. Marks search covers episodes with `lastPlayedAt`.
- **Debug launch options** (Debug builds only): `KIKU_ABS_URL` + `KIKU_ABS_KEY` and `KIKU_KAKU_URL` + `KIKU_KAKU_TOKEN` set up the servers (hand them in with `SIMCTL_CHILD_…`, never in code); `KIKU_OPEN_PAUSED=<item>/<episode>` opens the player without playing (nothing is saved until it plays); `KIKU_SEND_TO_WATCH=<item>/<episode>` sends it to the paired watch (docs/watch.md), and with `KIKU_SEND_FROM_PHONE=1` downloads it on the phone and uses Send from iPhone (the file only arrives on a real watch); `KIKU_WATCH_LIVE_TEXT=1` turns Live Text on Apple Watch on; `KIKU_IMPORT_DICTIONARIES="<zip>|…"`.
- **Tests:** `KikuTests` (follow logic and the marked line, new-episode counts, clips, environment, the offline library) and `KikuUITests` (`UITEST_KIKU=1`, in memory; `UITEST_KIKU_PLAYER=1` opens a seeded episode with silent local audio, also used to mark, look up and clear in Marks; `UITEST_STATS=1`). `AudiobookshelfKitTests` use scrubbed fixtures from 2.36.1.
- **Live checks** write to your Audiobookshelf: record an episode's progress (`GET /api/me/progress/{item}/{episode}`) before playing and PATCH it back after.
- New Episodes shows six on the Listen tab; See All has the rest. Settings ▸ Listening ▸ Libraries turns libraries on and off, like Yomu's and Miru's Libraries screens.
- **iPad and Mac sidebar:** the shown Audiobookshelf libraries are listed under the tabs (`SidebarLibraries` in AppCommon, as in Yomu). Each opens in the Listen tab as a Library screen with only that library's shows or books (`KikuEnvironment.requestedLibrary`, `ListenRoute.only`).
- **Transcript toggle**: the speech-bubble button (bottom right, where 追従 was) shows or hides the transcript, like Music's lyrics button, and the choice is kept for every episode (`ListenSettings.showsTranscript`, Core `v30_transcript_toggle`). Hidden, the player shows the artwork over a background tinted from it (`CoverTint`), the episode, and the line playing small and faint; tapping that line brings the transcript back. Scrolling the transcript by hand stops following and shows Back to Now, which resumes it. Tapping the current line still looks up its words.
