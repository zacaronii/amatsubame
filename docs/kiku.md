# Kiku (the audio app): working notes

Target and scheme `Kiku` (folder `Kiku/`, tests `KikuTests` and `KikuUITests`), `Kiku.app`, bundle `com.amatsubame.kiku`. Read this when working on Kiku. The plan is [AUDIO_PLAN.md](AUDIO_PLAN.md); shared notes are in [shared.md](shared.md); the transcriber is [kaku.md](kaku.md).

- **Tabs:** Listen (Today, Continue Listening, New Episodes, Library, show and book pages), Marks (marks to mine later, and search across your marks and played episodes' transcripts), Stats (the shared `StatsView`, kind `.listening`) and Settings.
- **Servers:**
  - **Audiobookshelf:** `Packages/AudiobookshelfKit` (`AudiobookshelfClient`). Sign in with a password (`/login` with `x-return-tokens: true`; the access token lasts an hour, the refresh token 30 days, both in the Keychain, refreshed once on a 401 by `AudiobookshelfTokens`) or a pasted API key (no refresh).
  - **Kaku:** `KakuClient` in the same package: URL and token in Settings. `POST /transcripts/status` gives the badges for a whole page in one request.
- **Audiobooks** (milestone 4): a book is one long "episode" with an empty id (`AudiobookshelfEpisode.wholeBook`, from the expanded item), keyed by its item id like Kaku keys it, so progress (`PATCH /api/me/progress/{item}`), transcripts, downloads and marks work as for podcasts. `EpisodeEntry.book` carries the item for chapters, the author (its `showTitle`) and the file count.
  - **Library:** `PodcastLibrary` loads book libraries too (`books`; `bookDetails` with chapters and tracks, saved as `book:<id>` for offline). Settings ▸ Libraries Shown in Kiku hides libraries (`ListenSettings.hiddenLibraries`, Core migration `v22_kiku_books`).
  - **Screens:** Audiobooks in the Library filter ("Audiobook · 38%"), books in Continue Listening and search, `BookView` (author · narrator, Resume/Play, download, chapters). The player shows the chapter under the title and has Chapters, Previous and Next Chapter in its more menu.
  - **Limits:** single-file books only for now (a multi-file book's page says so; Kaku already transcribes them on one timeline). MiscInfo is "Book — Chapter · 1:13:19".
- **Playback** (`Kiku/Playback`):
  - `AudioPlayer` (in AppCommon since milestone 5, shared with Yomu's read-along) wraps AVPlayer: the token goes in a header (`AVURLAssetHTTPHeaderFieldsKey`), never the URL. `.playback`/`.spokenAudio` session; Now Playing and remote commands (play/pause, skip back/forward with the Settings intervals, scrubbing). Artwork is built off the main actor: MediaPlayer asks for it on its own queue.
  - `PlaybackModel`: resume from the newer of local and Audiobookshelf progress, the transcript (saved copy, replaced quietly when Kaku's lines differ after a `kaku.resplit`; else Kaku: ready, waiting with `POST /jobs` to move it up, or missing), the current line (`TranscriptFollow`), line jumps, follow (追従), the sleep timer.
  - **Progress:** saved locally and queued every 15 s and on pause (`PATCH /api/me/progress/{item}/{episode}` through the outbox, coalesced per episode). A late report is dropped if Audiobookshelf has something newer.
  - **Listening time:** `ActivityLog` with kind `.listening`, only for real playback (not seeks).
- **Lookup and mining:** tapping a word pauses and opens `KikuLookupSheet` (the line with its clip, then the shared popup); closing resumes unless you pressed play yourself.
  - **Clips:** the line with 0.15 s before and 0.35 s after, AAC `.m4a`. Downloaded episodes and books are cut on the device with `AVAssetExportSession` (offline mining). It can't cut from a stream (`-11838`), so a streamed one asks Kaku's `/clips` first (`EpisodeAudio.remoteClip`, a few KB); when Kaku can't be reached (e.g. it's only on your home network) the whole file is fetched once to a temporary file and deleted when the player closes.
  - **Picture:** the episode cover. MiscInfo: "Show — Episode · 13:19" (`CardSource.miscInfo`).
- **Marks** (milestone 3, a prototype: mark now, mine later):
  - **Making one:** `KikuEnvironment.mark(_:)` saves the episode and the playing position (`AudioMarkStore`, Core migration `v21_kiku_marks`); nothing is recorded or downloaded. Four ways: the player's bookmark button; an AirPods triple press (milestone 6: previous track, through `AudioPlayer.onPreviousTrack`; a car's previous-track button does the same; a two-note `MarkChime` plays through the episode's audio session to confirm, since you aren't looking; a double press, next track, goes to the next line as in Miru); `MarkIntent` ("Mark in Kiku", an `AudioPlaybackIntent`, so it runs inside Kiku without opening it, for the Action Button, Siri, Back Tap and Shortcuts; it reaches the environment through `AppDependencyManager`). Now Playing's `bookmarkCommand` was tried and removed: iOS 26 shows it neither on the Lock Screen nor in Control Center.
  - **Action Button setup:** make a shortcut with Kiku's "Mark What I Just Heard" action in the Shortcuts app, then pick it under Settings ▸ Action Button ▸ Shortcut (or Back Tap). Its name is also a Siri phrase. It answers "Marked 13:21" or "Nothing is playing in Kiku". The built-in phrase "Mark in Kiku" may not work, probably because Siri doesn't recognise "Kiku"; a shortcut with your own name for it does.
  - **The Marks tab** (`MarksModel`): grouped by episode; each mark plays the 15 s before it and shows the line it's likely about (`TranscriptFollow.markedIndex`: the line that ended just before, unless the mark is 3 s or more into the line playing) with one line either side. Tapping a word looks it up and mines that line as in the player (`EpisodeAudio`, shared with `PlaybackModel`). `MiningContext.mined(_:)` records the word on the mark, and `MiningService.onCancelled` (Undo) clears it. A mined mark shows for a day, then is pruned. Marked episodes without a saved transcript are fetched from Kaku (and requested if missing); until then they show "Transcribing".
- **Downloads:** `EpisodeDownloader` (background URLSession, like Miru's): the audio, the cover and the transcript, in `Application Support/Downloads`. Settings ▸ Storage lists them by show.
- **Read in Yomu** (milestone 6, `Kiku/Listen/ReadInYomu.swift`): on every book page and in the player's ⋯ menu for a book. It pauses, saves the place, waits up to 3 s for the outbox to send it, then opens `amatsubame-yomu://read-along?item=<item id>&t=<seconds>`. Kiku can't see which books Yomu has paired (separate databases), so Yomu explains when one isn't. If Yomu isn't installed, an alert says so.
- **Saved transcripts:** `AudioTranscriptStore` (Core, migration `v19_kiku`): sentences with word timings and an FTS5 trigram index (LIKE under three characters), keyed `<item>/<episode>`. Marks search covers episodes with `lastPlayedAt`.
- **Debug launch options** (Debug builds only): `KIKU_ABS_URL` + `KIKU_ABS_KEY` and `KIKU_KAKU_URL` + `KIKU_KAKU_TOKEN` set up the servers (hand them in with `SIMCTL_CHILD_…`, never in code); `KIKU_OPEN_PAUSED=<item>/<episode>` opens the player without playing (nothing is saved until it plays); `KIKU_IMPORT_DICTIONARIES="<zip>|…"`.
- **Tests:** `KikuTests` (follow logic and the marked line, new-episode counts, clips, environment, the offline library) and `KikuUITests` (`UITEST_KIKU=1`, in memory; `UITEST_KIKU_PLAYER=1` opens a seeded episode with silent local audio, also used to mark, look up and clear in Marks; `UITEST_STATS=1`). `AudiobookshelfKitTests` use scrubbed fixtures from 2.36.1.
- **Live checks** write to your Audiobookshelf: record an episode's progress (`GET /api/me/progress/{item}/{episode}`) before playing and PATCH it back after.
- New Episodes shows six on the Listen tab; See All has the rest. Settings ▸ Listening ▸ Libraries turns libraries on and off, like Yomu's and Miru's Libraries screens.
- **iPad and Mac sidebar:** the shown Audiobookshelf libraries are listed under the tabs (`SidebarLibraries` in AppCommon, as in Yomu). Each opens in the Listen tab as a Library screen with only that library's shows or books (`KikuEnvironment.requestedLibrary`, `ListenRoute.only`).
- **Transcript toggle**: the speech-bubble button (bottom right, where 追従 was) shows or hides the transcript, like Music's lyrics button, and the choice is kept for every episode (`ListenSettings.showsTranscript`, Core `v30_transcript_toggle`). Hidden, the player shows the artwork over a background tinted from it (`CoverTint`), the episode, and the line playing small and faint; tapping that line brings the transcript back. Scrolling the transcript by hand stops following and shows Back to Now, which resumes it. Tapping the current line still looks up its words.
