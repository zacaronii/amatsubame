# Yomu (the reader): working notes

Target and scheme `Yomu` (folder `Yomu/`, tests `YomuTests` and `YomuUITests`), `Yomu.app`, bundle `com.amatsubame.yomu`: Kavita, the manga and EPUB readers, OCR and read-along. Read this when working on Yomu. Shared notes are in [shared.md](shared.md).

- Yomu UI tests (live Kavita, read-only; skipped without the env vars): pass `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_KAVITA_URL` and `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_KAVITA_KEY` (values from your gitignored `.env`, read literally, not `source`d, since keys and passwords may contain shell characters) to `xcodebuild test -scheme Yomu -destination '<iPhone or iPad simulator>' -only-testing:YomuUITests`
- OCR models for tests: `scripts/fetch-ocr-models.sh` (into gitignored `Models/`), then `OCR_MODELS_DIR=…/Models swift test` in `Packages/OCR`; add `OCR_PAGES_DIR=<folder of page images>` for the real-page report (writes annotated images; never commit pages).
- OCR UI tests also need `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_OCR_MODELS=<folder containing compiled/>` plus `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_DICTIONARY_ZIPS`, `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_LIBRARY` and `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_SERIES` (a Japanese series).
- Book UI tests (`BookUITests`): add `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_BOOK_LIBRARY` and `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_BOOK_SERIES` (a library and an EPUB series in it) to the Kavita env; `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_DICTIONARY_ZIPS` enables the lookup test.
- Book reader JS can be checked outside the app with a small WKWebView harness that injects `book.css`, `TextTapBridge.js` and `BookReader.js` into an unpacked chapter (off-screen views don't run animation frames, so don't rely on rAF).
- Reader debug launch options: `READER_DOWNLOAD_CHAPTERS=<chapterId>:<seriesId>,…` queues those downloads (from the library cache); `READER_REMOVE_DOWNLOADS=<chapterId>,…` or `=automatic` removes them (cleanup after live checks).
- Page turning:
  - **How the pager is built:** it's an eager HStack; pages more than `PagedReaderView.builtAround` (2) from the current one are placeholders.
  - **Zoom view:** its pan is off at 1×.
  - **Decoded pages:** kept in `PageImageView.decoded`, and the spinner waits 0.3 s.
  - **Test:** `testTurningPatternsAlwaysLandOnAPage`, needing the Kavita env plus `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_DECODE_DELAY_MS=150` for device-like decoding. Debug builds add "aligned" or "part-way" to the reader's value.
  - **Recording:** record the simulator (`xcrun simctl io <udid> recordVideo`) and check for black flashes with `swift scripts/analyze-page-turns.swift <mov>`. The pinch step and the reader opening show up as expected black frames.
- **Book progress in characters:** Kavita's figures are wrong for Japanese books.
  - **Why:** its word count is letters ÷ 5 (Kavita #4805, discussion #4809), and its pages are sections of very different lengths.
  - **How Yomu counts:** every book number comes from Yomu's own counts, done Hoshi Reader's and ッツ's way: letters only (kana, kanji, ー, 々, Latin, full-width and Korean letters and digits), without punctuation, spaces or furigana. The code is `BookTextIndex.count(html:)` and the `countedCharacter` class in `BookReader.js`. Counts match Hoshi Reader's for the same book.
  - **Where counts are kept:** each EPUB volume's count per section goes in `bookLength` (Core `v27_book_length`, `BookLengths`), saved whenever a book opens or by `BookLengthCounter`.
  - **The counter:** it counts from Kavita's `GET /api/Book/{chapterId}/book-page?page=N` (a section's HTML, no images, 0-based like the spine; fetching doesn't change progress), keeps only the numbers, and is started from Settings ▸ Character Counts.
    - **What it counts:** light novel libraries in full, plus volumes Kavita tags `ja` in other book libraries.
    - **After the first count:** new or changed volumes of counted series are counted after a library refresh on unmetered networks, and a series page counts its own missing volumes. A volume counts as changed when Kavita's pages or word count changed.
  - **Display:** series tiles, the series page, volume bars and rings, and Continue Reading use `BookCharacters` (in `ChapterNaming.swift`). A volume not counted yet is estimated from Kavita's word count × 5, shown with "~".
  - **What Kavita is still used for:** only the place between devices (section and paragraph). A section from another device counts as everything before it until the volume is opened here.
  - **Saved places:** they were cleared in `v26_counted_letters`, so each opens once from its xpath and then saves in the new count.
- **Read-along** (milestone 5, docs/AUDIO_PLAN.md): an EPUB volume paired with an Audiobookshelf audiobook plays with the text.
  - **Servers:** Settings ▸ Servers has Audiobookshelf and Kaku (AppCommon's `AudiobookshelfSettingsView`/`KakuSettingsView`, signed in separately from Kiku: servers are per app). Yomu's outbox also sends `AudiobookshelfProgressHandler` jobs.
  - **Pairing** (`Yomu/ReadAlong/ReadAlongViews.swift`): the series page of an EPUB series has a Read-Along section; a volume is paired with a book (suggested by title), stored in `ReadAlongStore` (Core `v23_read_along`).
  - **Matching, on the device:** `BookTextCollector` loads each spine item once in a hidden `BookWebController` and keeps `BookReader.js` `wordRuns()` (cached as `.read-along-runs-v1.json` in the book folder), so positions are (section, run, UTF-16 offset), the same as word marks. `ReadAlongAligner` matches them with Kaku's transcript (unique 6-character anchors, the longest chain in order, interpolation; kana and width folded) into `ReadAlongCues`, saved with the pair. On the novel it was tested with, 82% of the book's sentences matched (front matter, contents and the afterword aren't narrated), in about a minute in the simulator.
  - **In the reader:** `ReadAlongModel` (an `AudioPlayer` on the stream or Yomu's own download in `Application Support/ReadAlongAudio`) highlights the spoken sentence with `bookReader.readAlong.show` (a `<km-ra>` wrapper, like `<km-w>`) and turns the page or opens the next section to follow; paging or scrolling by hand (playing or paused) stops following and shows Follow Audio; playing again follows. The audio bar comes and goes with the reader's controls (a tap in the middle of the page); Follow Audio and From Here show either way. The headphones button in the reader's top bar turns read-along off for the volume (plain reading: no audio, highlight or Follow Audio; `ReadAlongPair.readsAlong`, Core `v24_read_along_off`) and on again from the page you're on; Kiku's Read in Yomu turns it back on. Turning it on from the switch listens from the page on screen: the last matched sentence at or before its first character (`firstVisiblePlace`, `ReadAlongCues.startIndex`). Reopening a book on the same device uses its own place to the character (`LocalReadingProgress.characters`; `BookReader.js` `charactersBeforePage`/`pageOfCharacter`), since a page can start inside a paragraph whose xpath (Kavita's position) is a page earlier. Hiding the status bar with the controls doesn't move the text (`BookReaderModel.setSafeArea` keeps the larger inset until the screen size changes). A lookup pauses and offers From Here, with the sentence's clip above the popup (`ReadAlongClipRow`, like Kiku's); its card gets the sentence's audio (cut from the download, or Kaku's `/clips` while streaming). Kavita progress follows the turned pages as usual; Audiobookshelf's goes through the outbox. On opening, if Audiobookshelf moved after the volume was last read (listening in Kiku), the book opens at that sentence.
  - **The bar** sits in the reader's stack (`BookReaderView`'s accessory), not an overlay, and its buttons and capsule have tap shapes: otherwise taps went through to the page and toggled the controls.
  - **From Kiku** (milestone 6): Yomu handles `amatsubame-yomu://read-along?item=<Audiobookshelf item>&t=<seconds>` (`AppEnvironment.open(_:)`, `CFBundleURLTypes` in `Yomu/Resources/Info.plist`). It finds the paired volume (`ReadAlongStore.pairs(itemId:)`), builds the session from the library cache (`KavitaLibraryRepository.cachedSeriesId(chapterId:)`), and hands the time to read-along (`linkedListening`; saving it as listening progress didn't work, because the reader closing saves its own place over it). A reader already showing switches in place (`requestedReader`); otherwise the root presents it. An unpaired book gets an alert.
  - **Screen:** while read-along plays, the screen doesn't dim (`ScreenAwake`, AppCommon; also Miru's playback).
  - **Live test:** `ReadAlongUITests` (in memory, nothing sent; `testReaderKeepsItsPlace` checks the page holds through the controls, the switch and a reopen) needs the Kavita env plus `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_ABS_URL/KEY`, `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_KAKU_URL/TOKEN`, `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_READALONG_LIBRARY` and `_SERIES` (a Kavita library and an EPUB series paired with an Audiobookshelf audiobook), `_ITEM` (the audiobook's id) for the link check, and optionally `_CHARACTERS` (the book's character count as shown, e.g. `12,345`) to check the count exactly. The link check opens it through `XCUIDevice.shared.system.open` (`app.open` relaunches the app and loses its in-memory data). Queries on a book page are slow (its text is in the accessibility tree), about 20 s each.
- **Progress bar** (book and manga): `ReaderProgressBar` runs along the screen's very bottom edge (ignoring the safe area), across both facing pages, stopping where the screen's rounded corners cross it (`ReaderProgressBar.cornerRadius`: a table of device screen sizes, 40 pt for others, none on the Mac; no private API). Always-on progress text sits just above it (under the first facing page), in the bottom inset where there's room (`BookReaderModel.jsSettings`' `textRoom`).
- **Facing pages on the iPhone Duo** (the foldable; see the iPhone Duo notes in [shared.md](shared.md)): `SpreadFrames`, `BookReaderModel.spread` with its `companion` web view, and `BookReader.js` `spread`/`follow`. A settings update sent while a section is still loading now replaces the settings it opens with (`BookWebController.update`); before, it was lost and the section opened with the old ones.
- The reader's model is made once per volume (`ReaderModelHost` in ReaderSession.swift). Making it in `body` gave the open reader a fresh, never-loaded model whenever the series page underneath redrew, e.g. on a download's progress (black screen with a spinner). Test: `testReaderStaysOpenWhileADownloadRuns`.
- **Controls and taps:** with the reader's controls showing, a tap on text only hides them (book and manga); the next tap looks up. The top bar covers the first characters of each column, so a word under it can't be seen whole.
- **iPad and Mac sidebar:** under the tabs it lists the shown Kavita libraries as buttons (`tabViewSidebarFooter`), each opening its series grid inside the Library tab (`AppEnvironment.requestedLibrary`). They aren't tabs: a picked tab in a `TabSection` makes the iPad's top bar add the section as a fifth tab. Tapping the tab that's showing starts it over from its top screen (the content's `.id` is bumped), as in Miru and Kiku.
