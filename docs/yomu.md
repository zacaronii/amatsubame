# Yomu (the reader): working notes

Target and scheme `Yomu` (folder `Yomu/`, tests `YomuTests` and `YomuUITests`), `Yomu.app`, bundle `com.amatsubame.yomu`: Kavita, the manga and EPUB readers, OCR and read-along. Read this when working on Yomu. Shared notes are in [shared.md](shared.md).

- Yomu UI tests (live Kavita, read-only; skipped without the env vars): pass `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_KAVITA_URL` and `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_KAVITA_KEY` (values from your gitignored `.env`, read literally, not `source`d, since keys and passwords may contain shell characters) to `xcodebuild test -scheme Yomu -destination '<iPhone or iPad simulator>' -only-testing:YomuUITests`
- OCR models for tests: `scripts/fetch-ocr-models.sh` (into gitignored `Models/`), then `OCR_MODELS_DIR=…/Models swift test` in `Packages/OCR`; add `OCR_PAGES_DIR=<folder of page images>` for the real-page report (writes annotated images; never commit pages).
- OCR UI tests also need `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_OCR_MODELS=<folder containing compiled/>` plus `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_DICTIONARY_ZIPS`, `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_LIBRARY` and `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_SERIES` (a Japanese series).
- Book UI tests (`BookUITests`): add `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_BOOK_LIBRARY` and `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_BOOK_SERIES` (a library and an EPUB series in it) to the Kavita env; `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_DICTIONARY_ZIPS` enables the lookup test.
- Book reader JS can be checked outside the app with a small WKWebView harness that injects `book.css`, `TextTapBridge.js` and `BookReader.js` into an unpacked chapter (off-screen views don't run animation frames, so don't rely on rAF).
- Reader debug launch options: `READER_DOWNLOAD_CHAPTERS=<chapterId>:<seriesId>,…` queues those downloads (from the library cache); `READER_REMOVE_DOWNLOADS=<chapterId>,…` or `=automatic` removes them (cleanup after live checks).
- Page turning:
  - **How the pager is built:** it's an eager HStack; pages more than `PagedReaderView.builtAround` (2) from the current one are placeholders.
  - **Zoom view:** its pan is off at 1×.
  - **Decoded pages:** kept in `PageImageView.decoded`, and the spinner waits 0.3 s.
  - **Test:** `testTurningPatternsAlwaysLandOnAPage`, needing the Kavita env plus `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_DECODE_DELAY_MS=150` for device-like decoding. Debug builds add "aligned" or "part-way" to the reader's value.
  - **Recording:** record the simulator (`xcrun simctl io <udid> recordVideo`) and check for black flashes with `swift scripts/analyze-page-turns.swift <mov>`. The pinch step and the reader opening show up as expected black frames.
- **Book progress in characters:** Kavita's figures are wrong for Japanese books.
  - **Why:** its word count is letters ÷ 5 (Kavita #4805, discussion #4809), and its pages are sections of very different lengths.
  - **How Yomu counts:** every book number comes from Yomu's own counts, done Hoshi Reader's and ッツ's way: letters only (kana, kanji, ー, 々, Latin, full-width and Korean letters and digits), without punctuation, spaces or furigana. The code is `BookTextIndex.count(html:)` and the `countedCharacter` class in `BookReader.js`. Counts match Hoshi Reader's for the same book.
  - **Where counts are kept:** each EPUB volume's count per section goes in `bookLength` (Core `v27_book_length`, `BookLengths`), saved whenever a book opens or by `BookLengthCounter`.
  - **The counter:** it counts from Kavita's `GET /api/Book/{chapterId}/book-page?page=N` (a section's HTML, no images, 0-based like the spine; fetching doesn't change progress), keeps only the numbers, and is started from Settings ▸ Storage ▸ Character Counts.
    - **What it counts:** light novel libraries in full, plus volumes Kavita tags `ja` in other book libraries.
    - **After the first count:** new or changed volumes of counted series are counted after a library refresh on unmetered networks, and a series page counts its own missing volumes. A volume counts as changed when Kavita's pages or word count changed.
  - **Display:** series tiles, the series page, volume bars and rings, and Continue Reading use `BookCharacters` (in `ChapterNaming.swift`). A volume not counted yet is estimated from Kavita's word count × 5, shown with "~".
  - **What Kavita is still used for:** only the place between devices (section and paragraph). A section from another device counts as everything before it until the volume is opened here.
  - **Saved places:** they were cleared in `v26_counted_letters`, so each opens once from its xpath and then saves in the new count.
- **Read-along** (milestone 5, docs/AUDIO_PLAN.md): an EPUB volume paired with an Audiobookshelf audiobook plays with the text.
  - **Servers:** Settings ▸ Servers has Audiobookshelf and Kaku (AppCommon's `AudiobookshelfSettingsView`/`KakuSettingsView`, signed in separately from Kiku: servers are per app). Yomu's outbox also sends `AudiobookshelfProgressHandler` jobs, and read-along listening goes to Audiobookshelf as listening sessions from "Yomu" (`AudiobookshelfSessionHandler`, the shared `ListeningSessionReporter`; docs/kiku.md ▸ Listening sessions).
  - **Pairing** (`Yomu/ReadAlong/ReadAlongViews.swift`): the series page of an EPUB series has a Read-Along section; a volume is paired with a book (suggested by title), stored in `ReadAlongStore` (Core `v23_read_along`).
  - **Matching, on the device:** `BookTextCollector` loads each spine item once in a hidden `BookWebController` and keeps `BookReader.js` `wordRuns()` (cached as `.read-along-runs-v1.json` in the book folder), so positions are (section, run, UTF-16 offset), the same as word marks. `ReadAlongAligner` matches them with Kaku's transcript (unique 6-character anchors, the longest chain in order, interpolation; kana and width folded) into `ReadAlongCues`, saved with the pair. On the novel it was tested with, 82% of the book's sentences matched (front matter, contents and the afterword aren't narrated), in about a minute in the simulator.
  - **In the reader:** `ReadAlongModel` (an `AudioPlayer` on the stream or Yomu's own download in `Application Support/ReadAlongAudio`) highlights the spoken sentence with `bookReader.readAlong.show` (a `<km-ra>` wrapper, like `<km-w>`) and turns the page or opens the next section to follow; paging or scrolling by hand (playing or paused) stops following and shows Follow Audio; playing again follows. The audio bar comes and goes with the reader's controls (a tap in the middle of the page); Follow Audio and From Here show either way. The headphones button in the reader's top bar turns read-along off for the volume (plain reading: no audio, highlight or Follow Audio; `ReadAlongPair.readsAlong`, Core `v24_read_along_off`) and on again from the page you're on; Kiku's Read in Yomu turns it back on. Turning it on from the switch listens from the page on screen: the last matched sentence at or before its first character (`firstVisiblePlace`, `ReadAlongCues.startIndex`). Reopening a book on the same device uses its own place to the character (`LocalReadingProgress.characters`; `BookReader.js` `charactersBeforePage`/`pageOfCharacter`), since a page can start inside a paragraph whose xpath (Kavita's position) is a page earlier. Hiding the status bar with the controls doesn't move the text (`BookReaderModel.setSafeArea` keeps the larger inset until the screen size changes). A lookup pauses and offers From Here, with the sentence's clip above the popup (`ReadAlongClipRow`, like Kiku's); its card gets the sentence's audio (cut from the download, or Kaku's `/clips` while streaming). Kavita progress follows the turned pages as usual; Audiobookshelf's goes through the outbox. On opening, if Audiobookshelf moved after the volume was last read (listening in Kiku), the book opens at that sentence.
  - **The bar** sits in the reader's stack (`BookReaderView`'s accessory), not an overlay, and its buttons and capsule have tap shapes: otherwise taps went through to the page and toggled the controls.
  - **From Kiku** (milestone 6): Yomu handles `amatsubame-yomu://read-along?item=<Audiobookshelf item>&t=<seconds>` (`AppEnvironment.open(_:)`, `CFBundleURLTypes` in `Yomu/Resources/Info.plist`). It finds the paired volume (`ReadAlongStore.pairs(itemId:)`), builds the session from the library cache (`KavitaLibraryRepository.cachedSeriesId(chapterId:)`), and hands the time to read-along (`linkedListening`; saving it as listening progress didn't work, because the reader closing saves its own place over it). A reader already showing switches in place (`requestedReader`); otherwise the root presents it. An unpaired book gets an alert.
  - **Screen:** while read-along plays, the screen doesn't dim (`ScreenAwake`, AppCommon; also Miru's playback).
  - **Live test:** `ReadAlongUITests` (in memory, nothing sent; `testReaderKeepsItsPlace` checks the page holds through the controls, the switch and a reopen) needs the Kavita env plus `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_ABS_URL/KEY`, `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_KAKU_URL/TOKEN`, `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_READALONG_LIBRARY` and `_SERIES` (a Kavita library and an EPUB series paired with an Audiobookshelf audiobook), `_ITEM` (the audiobook's id) for the link check, and optionally `_CHARACTERS` (the book's character count as shown, e.g. `12,345`) to check the count exactly. The link check opens it through `XCUIDevice.shared.system.open` (`app.open` relaunches the app and loses its in-memory data). Queries on a book page are slow (its text is in the accessibility tree), about 20 s each.
- **Progress bar** (book and manga): `ReaderProgressBar` runs along the screen's very bottom edge (ignoring the safe area), across both facing pages, stopping where the screen's rounded corners cross it (`ReaderProgressBar.cornerRadius`: a table of device screen sizes, 40 pt for others, none on the Mac; no private API). Always-on progress text sits just above it (under the first facing page), in the bottom inset where there's room (`BookReaderModel.jsSettings`' `textRoom`).
- **Facing pages on the iPhone Duo** (the foldable; see the iPhone Duo notes in [shared.md](shared.md)): `SpreadFrames`, `BookReaderModel.spread` with its `companion` web view, and `BookReader.js` `spread`/`follow`. A settings update sent while a section is still loading now replaces the settings it opens with (`BookWebController.update`); before, it was lost and the section opened with the old ones.
- The reader's model is made once per volume (`ReaderModelHost` in ReaderSession.swift). Making it in `body` gave the open reader a fresh, never-loaded model whenever the series page underneath redrew, e.g. on a download's progress (black screen with a spinner). Test: `testReaderStaysOpenWhileADownloadRuns`.
- **Controls and taps:** with the reader's controls showing, a tap on text only hides them (book and manga); the next tap looks up. The top bar covers the first characters of each column, so a word under it can't be seen whole.
- **iPad and Mac sidebar:** under the tabs it lists the shown Kavita libraries as buttons (`tabViewSidebarFooter`), each opening its series grid inside the Library tab (`AppEnvironment.requestedLibrary`). They aren't tabs: a picked tab in a `TabSection` makes the iPad's top bar add the section as a fifth tab. Tapping the tab that's showing starts it over from its top screen (the content's `.id` is bumped), as in Miru and Kiku.
- **Small conveniences:** the screen stays on while the reader is open (`ScreenAwake` counts its reasons, so read-along pausing doesn't undo it), until 15 minutes pass without the chapter's local progress moving (`ReaderContainer.staysAwake`). A library's grid on iPhone and iPad has the Mac's filter and sort as a menu (`libraryMenu`, AppCommon): Unread, Finished and the sort, beside the All · Reading · Downloaded picker (`LibraryFilterUITests` opens it against a real Kavita: `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_LIBRARY`).
- **Mark as Read / Unread** (the series page's volume and chapter menus): `AppEnvironment.setRead` saves this device's place and queues the same `kavitaProgress` job reading does (all pages, or none), and the page shows it at once (`KavitaSeriesContents.settingPagesRead`). When the page next loads, this device's place wins over Kavita's while it's the newer of the two (`readingFurther(_:updated:)` against `lastReadingProgressUtc`), so an unread mark doesn't bounce back before Kavita has it. **Time left in the chapter** (books): `BookTextIndex.charactersLeftInChapter` counts to where the next contents entry starts, shown before the book's time left.
- **Which character a tap hits (manga):** the recognizer gives a block's text and box, not character positions, so `TextLines.layout` measures them in the block's picture (a little more than the box, Otsu threshold, up to 1000 px; small blocks on low-resolution pages are doubled) and `OCRBlock.place` keeps the result (`lines`, `cells`: one rect per `Character` in the block's unit square, `cellsExact`, `measured` = `TextLines.version`; pages measured by an older version are measured again from the cached text when opened, no models needed).
  - **Ink:** only the lettering's: ink that runs off the picture's edge (the bubble's outline, art) and ink that doesn't touch the largest stretch of paper (screen tone past the outline in the corners) are cleared (`clearEdges`).
  - **Lines:** a projection across the lines. A full line's thickness comes from the runs' widths; runs thicker than that are parted at valleys (furigana stuck to its line, lines that touch), pieces of one line are joined, and a thin run within reach of a full line on its furigana side is furigana, not a line. Thin unbroken strokes and furigana lying past a line's end are left out.
  - **The grid:** Japanese lettering is set one character to a square, so one pitch is fitted for the whole block (each line with its own start): the pitch whose cuts cross the least ink (`grid`). If the pitch says the lines are thinner than first measured (furigana stuck to all of them), the lines are found again with it.
  - **Matching the text** (`placed`): a character per square, except what's drawn otherwise: a run of dots read as `．．．` is one … (or two) in a square, or a dot per square when the squares hold little ink; `！？` or two digits may share a square in a column (side by side); a long dash may take two. If the text fits the squares, the places are **exact**. If it fits once one stray line is left out (a sweat drop, a piece of outline), that line is dropped. Otherwise the places are a guess (the nearest squares, or for lettering not on a grid, Latin letters and hand-drawn titles, the glyphs by their gaps: `cellsByGaps`, exact only when every line has as many glyphs as characters).
  - **Use:** a tap goes to the nearest place, exact or guessed (`TextLayout.characterIndex(of:in:cells:)`; with no places at all, the even spread). The looked-up word is tinted on the page (`OCRTextLayer.marks`, reader settings ▸ Mark the Word on the Page) only where the places are exact: a mark that's a guess is worse than none. It follows taps in the popup's sentence (`MangaReaderModel.textOverlay`, setting `MangaReaderSettings.showsTextOverlay`, on by default). Nothing is typeset over the art (an earlier version did; the owner rejected it: it must sit on the lettering or not be there). `ReaderUITests.testLookupMarksTheWordOnThePage` runs it on real pages with screenshots.
  - **Checking on real pages:** `OCR_MODELS_DIR=… OCR_PAGES_DIR=<folder of pages> swift test --filter RealPageOCR` in `Packages/OCR` prints each block as exact or guessed and writes `annotated-*.png` with every character's place drawn (never commit pages). On 32 pages of three series (2026-11-22): 189 of 200 blocks exact; the rest were screen tone read as dots, hand lettering and sound effects.
- **Bookmarks and highlights on Kavita** (`ReaderMarks.swift` in Core, `KavitaMarks.swift` and `KavitaReaderMarkHandler.swift` in KavitaKit): every mark lives on Kavita; `readerMark` (v56) is the device's copy per account (`ReaderMarkStore.accountKey`) plus what's unsent (`state`: synced / pendingCreate / pendingUpdate / pendingDelete, `revision`). Kinds: manga page bookmarks (Kavita's `Reader/bookmark`, needs its Bookmark role; Kavita copies the page image into its bookmarks folder), book bookmarks (its personal table of contents, keyed by page and title) and highlights with notes (`Annotation`, Kavita 0.8.8+, five colour slots). Endpoint notes are at the top of `KavitaMarks.swift`.
  - **Sending:** each change writes the row and queues `kavitaReaderMark` with only the row's id (coalesced per mark) in the same write (`OutboxStore.enqueue(_:kind:coalesceKey:in:)`, so a refresh never sees a pending row without its job), so the handler sends the row as it is then: edits fold into an unsent make, a mark removed before it was sent never reaches Kavita, an edit that raced a make goes next (`ReaderMarkStore.didSend` compares `revision`). Kavita answers 400 (not 404) for an update or delete of an annotation that's gone; the handler checks `GET Annotation/{id}` first (and again after a 400): 404 means removed elsewhere, so an update makes it again once and a delete is done (a 404 on the delete itself is done too). A book bookmark made again while Kavita still has it gets "Duplicate bookmark entry already exists" (400): found in the list, it counts as made. Page bookmarks accept a repeat. A 404 on a highlight write is only "no annotations" if the list route says so; if the list can't be reached the job is tried again.
  - **What a server can't do** (`kavitaFeature`): a 404 from the annotation list (before 0.8.8) or a refusal of page bookmarks for an account without the Bookmark role marks the feature unavailable for that account, so nothing waits to sync for ever; unsent page bookmarks go (nothing typed), unsent highlights stay (below); the reader and the series page stop offering it and say why.
  - **Refreshing** (`KavitaReaderMarkSync`, when a series page loads and when the manga reader opens): page bookmarks by series, annotations by series (only this user's: the lists include other users' when they share theirs and this user views them), book bookmarks per volume. `ReaderMarkMerge.plan` applies the server's list (a change whose job was cleared in Waiting to Sync counts as synced, so the server's copy wins for it): its copy replaces synced rows, a synced row it lacks was removed elsewhere, unsent changes stay, an edit to a mark removed elsewhere makes it again, and a page bookmarked on two devices is one.
  - **Who sees annotations** is the user's own Kavita setting (Share Annotations, off by default); Yomu sends nothing about it and leaves the spoiler flag off.
  - **UI:** the manga reader's bookmark button (`PageBookmarks`, `reader.bookmark`) and the series page's Bookmarks & Highlights (`ReaderMarksSection`: tap opens the reader at the page, and in a book its place, via `ReaderSession.startPage`/`startXPath`, hold to remove). Books (`BookMarks`, `HighlightBar`): the bar above the lookup popup highlights the tapped word's sentence in one of Kavita's five colour slots, writes a note (`NoteEditor`; sent as `commentHtml` paragraphs) or removes it; the book bookmark button in the controls keeps the place on screen (`currentXPath`, titled by its first words, unique per page since Kavita deletes by page and title). In `BookReader.js`: `sentenceAt`/`markAt` read the sentence and highlight at the tapped point, captured when the tap arrives (the section can lay out again while the popup opens, and the point would then be elsewhere); `showUserMarks` wraps each highlight in `<km-hl>` by finding its text (whitespace ignored, furigana skipped) between its start and end elements, drawn as boxes outside the body in paged layouts like read-along (`placeUserMarks`); the start and end elements skip the reader's own wrappers so the XPaths are Kavita's. `BookMarksScriptTests` (macOS web view) covers the script.
  - **Live UI tests:** `ReaderUITests.testPageBookmarkReachesKavitaAndOpensFromTheSeries` and `BookUITests.testHighlightNoteAndBookmarkReachKavita` run against the test account with `UITEST_SENDS_MARKS` (marks are sent, progress isn't). `KavitaTestAccount` (YomuUITests) takes a snapshot of the account's annotations, page bookmarks and the book's bookmarks first, and teardown removes whatever was added, so a run that fails halfway leaves nothing. With `DEV_HOLD_PROGRESS` marks stay queued too.
  - **Opening from a mark** (the series list) starts the reader at its page (and in a book, its XPath) without recording progress: only a page turn does, so closing again leaves the reading position as it was, on the device and on Kavita.
  - **Notes and Kavita's formatting:** an update is sent over Kavita's current copy (fetched first), so a colour change keeps a note formatted in Kavita's web reader; changing the note's text in Yomu replaces it with plain paragraphs. An empty note (spaces only) is no note.
  - **Known limit:** a highlight is found again by its text between its start and end elements; if the same text occurs twice there, the first is marked.
  - **Signing in** to a server (any app, `ServerSettingsModel.saveAccount`) makes what waited for it due at once, rather than after its backoff (marks of another Kavita account wait while it's signed out).
  - **Unavailable annotations keep notes:** a server found without annotations keeps unsent highlights as rows without jobs (shown "Not on Kavita yet"; they survive restarts and signing in again to the same account), and a refresh that finds annotations queues them again. Only a 404 from the list route decides it; a 404 on a single write is that job's failure. A mark made under one Kavita account waits while another is signed in. Bookmarking a page again before its removal was sent takes the same row back, so a retried removal can't follow the make.
- **Want to Read on Kavita** (`WantToRead.swift` in Core, `KavitaWantToRead.swift` in KavitaKit): `wantToRead` (v57) is the device's copy per account (`ReaderMarkStore.accountKey`): Kavita's list in its order (`position`) and unsent toggles (`pendingAdd`/`pendingRemove`, `revision`). A toggle saves at once and queues `kavitaWantToRead` (`WantToReadJob`, coalesced per account and series) in the same write; the handler sends only rows still `pendingAdd`/`pendingRemove` (a job run again after the app died mid-way finds its row settled), sent as `add-series`/`remove-series` (both idempotent on Kavita). While another Kavita account is signed in, a waiting toggle is neither shown nor sent: its job waits, and signing back in sends it at once.
  - **Refresh** (`KavitaWantToReadSync`, with the Library home): the whole `want-to-read/v2` list, page by page (100 a page, up to 200 pages), merged so toggles still waiting stay (a pending row whose job was cleared in Waiting to Sync, or is gone, follows Kavita again; a failed job still listed keeps it), and so do toggles Kavita took after the list was fetched (`listedAt`; a sent removal stays as a `removed` row until a later list no longer has the series); its series are cached (`KavitaLibraryRepository.remember`) so the shelf works offline. The series page asks `GET want-to-read?seriesId` when it opens, which fills in only what isn't pending.
  - **Older Kavita:** the list route is in 0.7.8 and later (checked against the release tags; add, remove and the check are in 0.7.5). Its 404 marks `kavitaFeature.wantToRead` unavailable and drops unsent toggles (nothing typed); a 404 on one toggle with the list answering is that job's failure, and a list that can't be reached is retried.
  - **Shelf:** after Continue Reading, filtered like it (libraries Yomu reads, none hidden in Settings, offline only downloaded series), and absent when empty. `ReaderUITests.testWantToReadReachesKavitaAndTheShelf` adds and removes on the test account through the app; `KavitaTestAccount` restores Want to Read both ways.
- **Reader taps and rotation:** `ReaderTapZones` (standard, inverted, off) in both readers' settings (`MangaReaderSettings.tapZones`, `BookReaderSettings.tapZones`). Manga's Double-Tap to Zoom (`doubleTapZooms`, off by default) makes the page's tap gesture a double tap exclusively before the single one (`ZoomablePage`), zooming through `ZoomControl` into the native zoom view. Lock Rotation (`OrientationLock`, iOS; kept in UserDefaults) holds the orientation showing while a reader is open: Yomu's app delegate returns its mask. UI tests preset the taps with `UITEST_READER_TAPS`.
