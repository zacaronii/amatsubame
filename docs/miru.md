# Miru (the player): working notes

Target and scheme `Miru` (folder `Miru/`, tests `MiruTests` and `MiruUITests`), `Miru.app`, bundle `com.amatsubame.miru`. Read this when working on Miru. Shared notes are in [shared.md](shared.md).

- **Overview:**
  - Tabs: Watch (Jellyfin browsing, mpv playback with tappable Japanese subtitles), Lines (watched episodes' transcripts, search, live feed of other devices' playback) and Settings.
  - **The engine** (docs/AETHER_PLAN.md, phase 1): `PlaybackModel`, `PlaybackView`, `ExternalDisplay` and the AirPlay stand-in talk to the `PlayerEngine` protocol (PlayerKit), never to mpv directly; `MPVController` is the one conformance, passed to `PlaybackModel.init` (tests pass a fake). Its tracks are `PlayerTrack`, the AirPlay player a `PlayerStandIn`, the picture `videoView(fills:)`, the stats sheet's decoding rows `streamInfo`. **Pause After Each Line** asks the engine to call back at a time (`setTimeBoundary`): each time step the model either pauses now (the line showing ends within 0.12 s × speed, also right after a seek into that stretch) or sets the boundary at that point of the line showing, and checks again when it fires that no other line carries on past it. mpv checks the boundary on each of its time events, so it pauses on the same frame as before (0 ms past a frame-aligned target at 1× and 2×; between frames, the next frame: `LinePauseTimingTests` with `MPV_PAUSE_TIMING_VIDEO=<file> UITEST_MUTE=1`); an engine with a precise clock pauses on time.
  - `Packages/Player` (module `PlayerKit`): `MPVController`/`MPVVideoView` (MPVKit, LGPL), `MediaClipper` (FFmpeg: card audio and screenshots; `MediaClipper.card` makes both from one open, since opening a fansub MKV over the network reads its embedded fonts first, ~25 MB; before seeking it reads the x264 version from the first video frame and hands it to the decoder, since FFmpeg undoes old x264's 4:4:4 bug only once it has seen that note, #43), `SubtitleTrack` (SRT).
- Debug launch options (Debug builds only): `PLAYER_OPEN_ITEM=<Jellyfin item id>` opens that item straight away (`PLAYER_OPEN_AT=<seconds>` starts there, e.g. near the end for Up Next); `PLAYER_DOWNLOAD_ITEM=<id>` starts downloading it; `PLAYER_IMPORT_DICTIONARIES="<zip>|…"` imports dictionaries (on Mac the zips must be inside the app's container, e.g. `~/Library/Containers/com.amatsubame.miru/Data/tmp/`); `MPV_SMOKE=1` with `MPV_SMOKE_URL`/`MPV_SMOKE_AUTH` plays one stream (`MPV_CLIP=1` tests the clipper; `REAL_CLIP_URL`/`REAL_CLIP_AUTH`/`REAL_CLIP_AT` run `RealClipTests` in `Packages/Player` against a real stream); `MPV_LOG=v` raises mpv's log level. UI tests: `UITEST_PLAYER=1`, plus `UITEST_TRANSCRIPT=1` for a saved transcript; with the local test video, `UITEST_PLAYER_START=<s>` opens it part-way in and `UITEST_PLAYER_FOLLOWING=<path>` gives it a next episode (both for the first video only).
- Live checks write to your Jellyfin: record the item's UserData first and restore it after (`POST /UserItems/{id}/UserData`). Close player windows (stop report) before restoring, or a lingering session writes its last position back when it times out. Another device can be faked with a script posting `/Sessions/Playing` + `/Progress` under a dev token (it can't receive remote Pause: Jellyfin only reports `SupportsRemoteControl` for clients with a websocket).
- Playback reports (`PlaybackModel.report`):
  - **What's sent:** a report every 10 s while playing or paused, with Pause/Unpause/TimeUpdate event names, AudioStreamIndex/SubtitleStreamIndex and `reportedAt`, plus a report right after a seek. Only time updates coalesce in the outbox.
  - **Why so often:** Jellyfin ends a session it hasn't heard from in a few minutes, and Playback Reporting only processes a progress report 20 s after the last one it used.
  - **Leaving the screen:** while playing, audio continues in the background (`UIBackgroundModes: audio`, mpv video off via `setVideoEnabled`) and so does the session. While paused (or paused from AirPods or the Lock Screen while away), the session ends with a stop; play from those controls starts a new one. Quitting on Mac ends it.
  - **Coming back:** the session restarts. If the episode was watched further elsewhere meanwhile, the player moves there, still paused, with a note.
  - **Late reports:** the handler drops starts and progress older than 2 minutes, and sends an old stop only if Jellyfin has nothing newer.
- Up Next:
  - **Next episode:** the player knows it from `session.following`, or else from the series' episode list (`JellyfinUpNext.episodes(after:)`).
  - **The card:** it shows when the credits segment starts, or 20 s before the end, and plays after a 10 s countdown. Pausing holds the countdown; Cancel hides the card for that episode. When the countdown runs out it starts `playNext` from a new task, because stopping the countdown cancels its own task.
  - **Last episode or a movie:** the end card offers `JellyfinUpNext.suggestions` from `/Items/{id}/Similar`, from the same library first and never hidden libraries. Tapping one opens it in the Watch tab (`PlayerEnvironment.openSeries`).
- **iPad and Mac sidebar:** the shown Jellyfin libraries are listed under the tabs (`SidebarLibraries` in AppCommon, as in Yomu), from the list the Watch tab last loaded (`PlayerEnvironment.libraries`). Each opens its whole library in the Watch tab, through `openSeries`.
- Now Playing (`Miru/Playback/NowPlaying.swift`):
  - **Commands:** AirPods press plays or pauses, double-press goes to the next line, triple-press goes back to the line before the one on screen (`SubtitleTrack.lineBefore`; swipes keep the music-player rule of `previous(before:)`); the Lock Screen and Control Center scrubber seeks; the Mac media key works too.
  - **Audio session:** set before mpv loads (setting it later disturbed playback). It must never mix with others: a mixing app doesn't get the AirPods, Lock Screen or Control Center controls.
    - **mpv:** its iOS audio output (`ao_audiounit`) sets the session itself each time audio starts, with mix-with-others unless `audio-exclusive=yes`. `MPVOptions.audioSession` sets that on iOS only (on the Mac it would take over the audio device).
    - **Word audio** (`Lookup/AudioService`) leaves a `.playback` session as it is.
    - **Test:** Debug builds add "mixing" to `player.video`'s value when the session mixes, and `testBackgroundAudioAndNowPlaying` checks for it.
  - **Diagnostic log:** `Application Support/nowplaying.log` in Miru's container records the session, the info set and the commands received. Read it with `devicectl … --domain-type appDataContainer --domain-identifier com.amatsubame.miru`. The simulator's Control Center has no Now Playing module.
  - **Test:** `testBackgroundAudioAndNowPlaying` needs `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_PLAYER_AUDIO_VIDEO`, a test video with an audio track.
  - **Threads:** handlers and artwork may run off the main thread.
  - **Quitting on Mac:** the stop is queued and sent at the next launch, so a live check should close the player window (Escape) before quitting, or relaunch afterwards.
- Jellyfin `/Sessions` already advances a playing session's PositionTicks between client reports (whole seconds, lagging by up to 1 s), so never add the time since `LastPlaybackCheckIn` on top. `LiveClock` keeps the best reading.
- Intro/credits skipping uses `GET /MediaSegments/{itemId}`, cached in the `mediaSegment` table. UI tests fake segments with `UITEST_PLAYER_SEGMENTS="Intro:0:30"` on the local test video.
- **Chapters** (#178): `PlaybackModel.chapters` is Jellyfin's item `Chapters` (`JellyfinChapter`, on `item(id:)` by default; checked on 12.4.0, where several can share a start), or mpv's own `chapter-list` (`MPVController.fileChapters`) for a download or local file. `PlayerChapters` (PlayerKit) keeps one per start (the last of several: a prologue and an opening both at 0 are the opening), drops any past the end, and has nothing below two. The scrubber draws a mark at each start after the first, where the thumb's centre sits at that time (`SliderGeometry`: the platform slider's own thumb rect at its minimum, `UISlider.thumbRect` / `NSSliderCell.knobRect`, for the scrubber's size; on iOS 26 a 37 pt thumb, so 18.5 pt in from each end, measured on screen to within half a pixel), the scrub preview names the chapter, and `PlayerShortcut.previousChapter`/`.nextChapter` (Page Up/Down; on iOS through hidden key-command buttons, as iOS gives those keys only to key commands) and the options menu move between them: previous goes to the chapter's start after 3 s in, else the one before. UI test: `testChaptersFromTheOptionsMenu` with `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_PLAYER_CHAPTERS_VIDEO` (a 60 s video with chapters at 0, 15 and 40 s; ffmpeg `-map_chapters` from an FFMETADATA file).
- Kaku subtitles (milestone 6) for episodes Jimaku has nothing for: Settings ▸ Watching ▸ Subtitles ▸ Kaku (its own sign-in, `AudioServers.kakuClient`), then the Subtitles sheet's **Transcribe with Kaku** (or **the Rest of the Season**: the later episodes of the same season, in order) calls `POST /jellyfin/jobs` ([kaku.md](kaku.md)). The sheet shows the queue place or progress (polled every 5 s). The SubRip is saved as the episode's `localSubtitle` with `source: "kaku"` ("From Kaku"), so lookup, Auto-Sync, Save to Jellyfin… and offline work as for a Jimaku file; a Jimaku pick replaces it. Opening an episode with no Japanese subtitles on Jellyfin or the device checks Kaku and uses a finished transcript. Kaku transcribes a 24-minute episode in about 30 s on a mid-range consumer GPU.
- The screen stays on while a video plays (`ScreenAwake`): mpv doesn't keep it awake like Apple's player does.
- Jimaku subtitles (`Packages/JimakuKit`):
  - The API key is in the Keychain (`SecretKey.jimakuAPIKey`, Settings → Servers → Jimaku); `JIMAKU_API_KEY` in `.env` is for live checks.
  - Shows are found by AniList id, mapped from the season's AniDB id (when the library provides one, e.g. through Shokofin) through arm.haglund.dev; otherwise by name.
  - The picked file is stored per item in `localSubtitle` (it wins over Jellyfin's tracks), with the user's timing offset.
  - Nothing goes to Jellyfin by itself: "Save to Jellyfin…" in the timing panel (confirmed first) queues a `jellyfinSubtitle` outbox job (`POST /Videos/{id}/Subtitles`, base64, current timing). `LocalSubtitle.serverState` follows `editedAt`/`queuedAt`/`uploadedAt`.
  - Uploads send `Language: "ja"` (two-letter code): Jellyfin builds the file name from it, `<video name>.<language>[.forced][.sdh].<format>` (it never overwrites, adding `.0` instead). To remove a test upload: `DELETE /Videos/{id}/Subtitles/{index}`.
  - Auto-Sync (`PlayerKit/SubtitleSync`, alass's method in Swift): to the video's embedded text tracks first (`JellyfinSubtitlePicker.syncReferences`), then the downloaded English file, then the speech in the audio (`MediaClipper.speech` with libfvad from `Vendor/libfvad`, from the download or `JellyfinClient.audioOnlyStreamURL`, ~5 MB). Split penalty 20 and detector mode 1 were tuned on real episodes. The synced file replaces `data`; `originalData` keeps the download for Reset.
  - UI tests use `UITEST_JIMAKU=1` for canned answers and `UITEST_SYNC_REFERENCE=1` for a sync reference. In `Packages/Player`:
    - `REAL_SUBTITLE_FILES="<path>|…" swift test` parses real files;
    - `REAL_SYNC_PAIRS="<sub>><reference>|…"` syncs files to subtitle references;
    - `REAL_SYNC_AUDIO=<file or URL>` (with `REAL_SYNC_AUDIO_AUTH`) and `REAL_SYNC_AUDIO_SUBTITLES` sync to the audio. `REAL_SYNC_VAD_MODES` and `REAL_SYNC_PENALTIES` compare settings. Use `-c release`.
- Scrub previews: `ScrubThumbnails` uses Jellyfin trickplay if the item has it (only when the server is set to make it), else `MediaClipper.frame` from the download or the stream.
- Search (Watch ▸ the magnifying glass, `JellyfinSearchView`) is on the Watch tab's path as `JellyfinSearchRoute`, like the shows it opens: pushed outside the path, it was rebuilt in front when a result joined the path. `SearchUITests` checks it against a real server: Miru signed in on the simulator (`DevSetup`) and `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_SEARCH_QUERY`.
- **The options menu** (`PlaybackView.optionsMenu`, the sliders button in the top bar): speed (`PlaybackModel.speeds`), the audio track, English subtitles and Pause After Each Line, which were keys only. The controls don't hide while it's open. The speed chosen (menu or keys) is saved in `PlayerSettings.speed` and applied when a player starts. **Pause After Each Line** (`PlayerSettings.pausesAfterLines`, also the O key): `SubtitleTrack.lineEnding` finds the line within 0.12 s of its end (times the speed), and `timeChanged` pauses there once per line, so the line is still showing; going back clears it, so a replayed line pauses again. **English Subtitles** (`toggleEnglish`): the English file (a download's, or Jellyfin's subtitle URL for the English stream) is added to mpv once with `sub-add … select`, sent with `mpv_command_async`: Jellyfin pulls an embedded track out of the video file first, which on a large remux takes a while, and the synchronous command held the main thread that long (the switch then didn't answer). `noteEnglishTrack` remembers the track when it appears and keeps mpv's `sid` to the switch, so a file that arrives after English was turned off stays hidden. Tests: `testEnglishSubtitlesTurnOnAndOff` (on, off, on through the menu; `UITEST_PLAYER_ENGLISH_OFFERED` offers a file without showing it), `testEnglishTurnedOffWhileItLoadsStaysOff` (a slow server: the tap returns at once and English stays off), and the live `EnglishSubtitlesLiveUITests` (a streamed episode with embedded English). Debug builds add mpv's `sid` and current `sub-text` to the picture's accessibility value for them.
- **Condensed** (#178; the options menu and the C key, `PlayerShortcut.condensed`): `SubtitleTrack.silentStretches()` (PlayerKit, tested) gives the stretches with no Japanese line: before the first line, and between lines more than `condensedGap` (3 s) apart, from `condensedTail` (0.3 s) after a line to `condensedLead` (0.5 s) before the next; nothing after the last line. They're worked out from the subtitles as timed (a timing fix moves them). `PlaybackModel.timeChanged` seeks to a stretch's end when playback is inside it, playing, with no lookup open. Per show (`SeriesPlayerSetting`, Core `v68`, by series id or a movie's id; off by default, kept only when on). Off without Japanese subtitles. The slider darkens the stretches while it's on. Stats count only what played (seeks aren't counted). UI test: `testCondensedSkipsTheStretchesWithNoLines` (a local video and `UITEST_JIMAKU=gaps`).
- **The subtitles' look** (`PlayerSettings.subtitleScale`, `subtitleLift`, `concealsSubtitles`, `pausesOnHover`; the options menu's Subtitles submenu and Settings ▸ Subtitles): the player keeps a copy (`PlaybackModel.look`) so a change shows at once. Hidden lines are blurred and take no taps until one tap (or the pointer) shows them (`SubtitleOverlay.concealed`); the line being looked up always shows. On the Mac the pointer on the lines pauses quietly and resumes on leaving, unless a lookup is open. **Sleep timer** in the options menu: minutes, or the end of the episode (which holds Up Next back). UI tests set the look with `UITEST_PLAYER_LOOK="conceal,large,higher"`.
- **Hold for 2×** (iPhone, iPad): a long press on the picture (`PlayerGestures`, gesture state, so a cancelled touch lets go) calls `PlaybackModel.setHoldsFast`, which doubles mpv's speed without saving it and restores it on release; drags are ignored meanwhile. **Mark as Watched / Unwatched** (`WatchedMenuItem` in the episode rows' and home rows' menus): `PlayerEnvironment.setWatched` writes this device's progress and queues a `JellyfinPlayedPayload` (outbox kind `jellyfinPlayed`, newest per item wins); `JellyfinPlayedHandler` sends `POST` or `DELETE /Users/{id}/PlayedItems/{item}` (checked against the server, 2026-11-22) and the lists reload when it lands.
- **Pinch to fill** (iPhone, iPad; #178): a `MagnifyGesture` on the picture (`PlayerGestures`) past ±10 % (`PlayerGestureMath.fills(afterPinch:filling:)`) sets `PlaybackModel.fillsScreen` (with an OSD note; it lasts while the player is open, so Up Next's episodes, which reuse the model, stay filled, and a newly opened player starts fitted). It applies only in the `.overlay` layout (`PlayerLayout.canFill`: a phone held sideways), so the bars and split layouts keep their controls clear, and rotating to portrait fits the picture and back sideways fills it again. Filling is done by the video view, not mpv: `MPVHostView` sizes its Metal layer to the video shape and fits it (`videoFrame`), so mpv's own surface has no bars and pan-and-scan would do nothing. Filling, the layer is the smallest video-shaped rect covering the view, clipped by the view; mpv redraws at the new drawable size through the usual resize path. The app's Japanese subtitles are placed on the screen, not the picture, so they don't move; mpv's English track is drawn inside its (now larger) surface at the top, so the host view reports the share cropped at the top and at the sides (`onCrop`); `MPVController.setCrop(top:side:)` moves `sub-margin-y` down by the top share and `sub-margin-x` in by the side share (a picture wider than the screen, e.g. a 2.39:1 film on a phone) (checked on the simulator with `UITEST_PLAYER_ENGLISH=<srt>`, a Debug hook that loads an English file into the local test video). While pinching, the one-finger drag (scrub, brightness, volume) is dropped. VoiceOver: the picture's Zoom to Fill / Fit to Screen action.
- **Buffering (#143):** `MPVController` observes mpv's `paused-for-cache` and `cache-buffering-state` (`isBuffering`, `bufferPercent`). The player shows "Buffering 42%" once a wait has lasted 0.4 s (`PlaybackModel.bufferingChanged`; a seek refills in less), and each wait goes in the app log's `playback` area and the stream info (`StallLog`: "Waited 2.5 s for the stream at 12:34 (wait 1, 2.5 s in all)"). `BufferingUITests` needs `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_PLAYER_SLOW_VIDEO=http://127.0.0.1:<port>/…`: a video served from the Mac slower than it plays (Debug builds open such an address like a local test video). **Tuning:** mpv starts on `cache-pause-initial` and `cache-pause-wait` 2 s; `BufferWait` raises the wait (2 → 6 → 12 → 20 s of video) each time the stream runs dry again within 90 s of the last wait and lowers it after a longer clean run, ignoring waits within 3 s of a seek. `stream-lavf-o` reconnects a dropped connection. While buffering, `streamSpeed` (mpv's `cache-speed`) against `averageBitRate` (file size over length) gives the overlay's "Arriving at 0.6 Mbps / This video needs 2.8" (`PlaybackModel.slowStreamNote`). **Quality** (`PlayerSettings.maxStreamBitrate`, 0 = original; Settings ▸ Playback and the player's menu): when the file's bit rate is over the limit, `PlaybackModel.open` asks `JellyfinClient.transcodingInfo` (POST PlaybackInfo with a profile of no direct-play entries, HLS/TS H.264 + stereo AAC, `SubtitleStreamIndex -1` or the server burns the user's default subtitles in; checked on 10.11) and plays the `TranscodingUrl` through `transcodeURL`, which holds the audio to 96–192 kbps and gives the picture the rest. It gets its own play session; reports say `PlayMethod: Transcode`, and the stopped report is what ends the server's transcode. The audio is chosen up front (Japanese, else the default) since mpv then sees one track. Seeking works (the server restarts the transcode there). Changing quality ends the session and reopens at the same place (`chooseQuality`); the buffering box offers `PlayerSettings.streamQuality(fitting:)` when the stream is slower than the video (`lowerQualityOffer`). Thumbnails, sync audio and card clips still use the original. `StreamQualityUITests` plays a show transcoded against a real server (`UITEST_STREAM_QUALITY` overrides the setting for a run). **Hangs that aren't waits (#143):** once a second `PlaybackModel` feeds `StuckWatch` (PlayerKit) the engine's time while it should be moving (started, not paused, not buffering, not at the end, not in picture in picture); 3 s without moving writes "Stuck at 12:34 for 3.0 s (not paused, not waiting for the stream; stuck 1) [streamed as is]" to the app log, and "Was stuck … until moving again" when it ends; the stream info's "Stuck while playing" counts them. **Slow lookups:** a tap on a line whose popup takes 0.3 s or more logs "Slow lookup: the popup came … after the tap (paused after …, dictionary …)" with the player's state at the tap (`LineLookup.lookUp`'s `shown` gives the dictionary's share), so a hang in the pause (mpv's core) tells apart from one in the lookup (or the Hachidori host on the Mac).
- Gesture UI test: generate a local video (any H.264 .mp4) and pass `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_PLAYER_VIDEO=<path>` (Debug builds open a local path as the item). Rotation checks: `MPV_SMOKE=1` with `MPV_SMOKE_URL=<file>`, optional `MPV_SMOKE_PAUSED=1` and `MPV_LOG=v`; count mpv's "Window size" lines after Device → Rotate.
- Keyboard shortcuts: `Miru/Playback/PlayerShortcuts.swift` is the one list (keys, titles, sections) behind the player's key handler, the Playback menu and the cheat sheet (Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts on Mac and iPad, and `?` over the video). Most are mpv's; ↑/↓ stay previous/next line.
  - **Actions:** `PlaybackModel.perform(_:)`, with an mpv-style message top left (`osd`).
  - **Matching** (`PlayerShortcut.matching`, unit-tested in `PlayerShortcutTests`): Shift is ignored for typed characters, and iOS can report Shift-[ as "[" with Shift held, so that's mapped to "{". Backspace is DEL on the Mac and BS on iOS.
  - **Menu shortcuts:** on the Mac only. On iOS, menu key commands matched Shift-[ as [ some of the time.
  - **Esc and Backspace on iOS:** they reach the player only through hidden key-command buttons.
  - **Tests:** the simulator's `typeKey` drops Shift now and then and never sends Esc or Backspace, so `PlayerShortcutsUITests` avoids those. They were checked on the Mac by posting real key events to the app's pid (`CGEvent.postToPid`).
- mpv's built-in Lua scripts must all stay off (`MPVOptions.noScripts`): the Mac refuses LuaJIT's code. mpv 0.41's `context_menu` was missing and crashed Miru on the Mac. `MPVOptionsTests.everyBuiltInScriptIsKnown` reads the scripts inside libmpv and fails on a new one.
- **Lines' small states:** search shows "No Results" only once a search for what's typed has run (`LinesView.showsNoResults`; it flashed during the 200 ms debounce); Watching Now keeps its last list through dropped polls, with a "Can't reach Jellyfin" line, and clears after three failures in a row (`PollFailures`, about 30 s); a live feed's "Couldn't Load Subtitles" has **Try Again** (`TranscriptModel.load` again). Settings ▸ Subtitles ▸ Kaku is a destination push like the Subtitles screen it's on (a value push from a destination-pushed screen is the mix behind #145).
- **Lines history grouping:** episodes of one show watched back to back are one row (`LinesHistoryModel.runs`), opening to them in history order; a single episode stays a plain row, so Lines still reads as a watch history.
- **Karaoke build-ups:** a line that the next one starts with, straight after it (「恋」 then 「恋をしたから」), is folded into the finished line when Lines are saved (`TranscriptLines.mergingBuildUps`; Core `v28_merge_build_ups` fixed saved ones). On screen the build-up still plays as drawn.
- Recently Added shows titles under the posters.
- **Player layouts** (picked from the screen's shape, e.g. on the iPhone Duo): `PlayerLayout` (overlay, controls in the black bars, or the picture over `PlayerLinesStrip`), placed in screen coordinates by `PlaybackView.place`. The Mac always uses the overlay. Tests: `PlayerLayoutTests`.
- **External display** (#178; iOS): a connected screen (AirPlay mirroring, a cable) gets its own scene (`UIWindowSceneSessionRoleExternalDisplayNonInteractive`), routed by `MiruAppDelegate` (`@UIApplicationDelegateAdaptor`) to `ExternalDisplaySceneDelegate`, whose window shows `ExternalVideoView`. `ExternalDisplay.shared` (`Playback/ExternalDisplay.swift`) knows the screen is there and which player is open (`PlaybackView` sets it). While both hold, the player's picture area shows "Playing on the Other Screen" and the one mpv layer moves to the external window: `MPVHostView` starts from the layer's last size, so the move counts as a resize and mpv draws at the TV's size. Lines, controls and lookups stay on the device. AirPlay video to a TV goes through AVPlayer instead (below). Test: `testExternalDisplayKeepsTheLinesHere` (`UITEST_EXTERNAL_DISPLAY` simulates a screen; the simulator can't attach one).
- **AirPlay video** (iOS): `AirPlayButton` (`AVRoutePickerView`, `prioritizesVideoDevices`, labelled "AirPlay") picks the receiver. On `AVAudioSession.routeChangeNotification` (and when mpv starts, and back from the background) `PlaybackModel.airPlayRouteChanged` tries an AirPlay output once per route and video, unless a screen is mirrored (`ExternalDisplay` keeps mpv's picture), PiP is on, or the route is known to be a speaker (`airplay.soundOnlyRoutes`) or failed this run. `AirPlayVideoHandoff` (`Playback/AirPlayVideo.swift`) gets a source ready on `AVAirPlayPlayer` (an `AVPiPPlayer` with `allowsExternalPlayback`, `usesExternalPlaybackWhileExternalScreenIsActive = false`) through the same `PictureInPictureHandoff` and `avPlayerSources(item:bitrate:forTV:)` PiP uses, while mpv plays on; then the receiver must report `isExternalPlaybackActive` (paused, else playing muted) within 3 s, or it's a speaker and mpv keeps the sound. For a TV: the direct stream when the file plays as it is and there's no Jellyfin Japanese track to leave out, else the HLS transcode at up to 20 Mbps (`tvBitrate`, or Settings' streaming quality when lower) with `copiesVideo` (`AllowVideoStreamCopy`: an H.264 video within the limit is copied). With `SubtitleMethod=Hls` Jellyfin lists every text track as a WebVTT rendition (checked on 10.11: Japanese `DEFAULT=YES`, English there but off); `AVPiPPlayer` selects the Japanese one (`isJapanese`: "jpn" or "ja"), which also fixes PiP picking the first. Once the TV has it, `MPVController.attach(_:)` makes the AVPlayer mpv's stand-in (`MPVStandIn`): mpv pauses, `setPaused`/`seek`/`setSpeed` go to the TV, and its periodic time observer reports time, pause, waiting (`standInReport`), so the lines, current line, Pause After Each Line, Condensed, progress, Stats and Now Playing follow the TV with no other changes. `watch()` returns `.back` when the picture leaves the TV (another route picked: mpv takes over there, playing if the TV was) or `.unreachable` when the item fails or the time stands still for 15 s while playing (the TV can't reach the server: mpv takes over with the sound on the receiver and an OSD says so). The episode ending on the TV hands back at the end, so mpv's own end brings Up Next (the next episode goes to the TV again). Downloads: MKV says once that AirPlay plays only the sound. Tests: `AirPlayVideoHandoffTests` (fake player: TV, TV that waits for play, speaker, no source, unreachable, failure, paused), `MPVStandInTests` (PlayerKit), `aTVTranscodeCopiesTheVideo` (JellyfinKit), `testAirPlayButtonReachesTheRoutePicker` (the simulator has no AirPlay, so it checks the tap reaches the picker's button). The real check is an Apple TV.
- **Picture in picture** (iOS): mpv has no system PiP, so the sliders menu's item hands the episode to AVPlayer (`Playback/PictureInPicture.swift`: `AVPiPPlayer`, `PictureInPicture` around `AVPictureInPictureController`, its layer in the picture area via `PiPLayerHost`). `PictureInPictureHandoff` tries the sources in order and takes the first that gets ready: Jellyfin's static stream when the container and codecs suit AVPlayer (`playsDirectly`: MP4/MOV, H.264/HEVC, AAC/MP3/AC-3/E-AC-3/ALAC) and no lower quality is chosen, then an HLS transcode (`transcodingInfo(…, subtitleStreamIndex:)` pins mpv's audio track and puts the chosen Jellyfin text track in the playlist as WebVTT, `SubtitleProfiles [{vtt, Hls}]`). A failure goes straight to the next; all failing, or 30 s overall, leaves mpv playing. The transcode is at most 4 Mbps (`pipBitrate`): at a remux's own bitrate Jellyfin copies the video and a segment was about 20 MB, so the first segment and the one at the seek (Jellyfin restarts ffmpeg there) took longer than the hand-off allowed. mpv's video is off while PiP starts and plays (`setVideoEnabled(false)`): with it on, AVPlayer's item never reached ready on the iPad simulator. The Jellyfin session stays open while PiP plays: `movedToBackground` and a pause while away don't end it (`inPictureInPicture`), or the Stopped report made Jellyfin kill the stream PiP was playing (the owner's first test); progress and Stats follow AVPlayer meanwhile, and closing PiP while away and paused ends the session then. `AVPiPPlayer.prepare` loads the asset's `isPlayable`, then checks the item's status every 0.1 s (a KVO observation held only by the wait could be lost). Downloads play directly (not MKV, so the item is hidden). Back in the app (the player's "Back Here", or the window's restore button) mpv seeks to AVPlayer's time and plays if it was playing; the window's × leaves mpv paused there; the episode ending closes the window. Progress reports and Stats take AVPlayer's time meanwhile. Every transcode is stopped (`stopTranscoding`, `DELETE /Videos/ActiveEncodings`) when it isn't used or PiP ends; its play session is kept in UserDefaults (`pip.pendingTranscode`) so a kill mid-PiP stops it at the next launch (Jellyfin also ends idle transcodes on its own). A Jimaku/Kaku file or an un-uploaded timing fix can't go along: a one-time OSD note says so. AVKit drops a `startPictureInPicture()` sent before the controller's status settles (logs `status = 0`, no delegate call), so `start()` asks again every 0.4 s for up to 4 s.
