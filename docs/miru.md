# Miru (the player): working notes

Target and scheme `Miru` (folder `Miru/`, tests `MiruTests` and `MiruUITests`), `Miru.app`, bundle `com.amatsubame.miru`. Read this when working on Miru. Shared notes are in [shared.md](shared.md).

- **Overview:**
  - Tabs: Watch (Jellyfin browsing, mpv playback with tappable Japanese subtitles), Lines (watched episodes' transcripts, search, live feed of other devices' playback) and Settings.
  - `Packages/Player` (module `PlayerKit`): `MPVController`/`MPVVideoView` (MPVKit, LGPL), `MediaClipper` (FFmpeg: card audio and screenshots; `MediaClipper.card` makes both from one open, since opening a fansub MKV over the network reads its embedded fonts first, ~25 MB; before seeking it reads the x264 version from the first video frame and hands it to the decoder, since FFmpeg undoes old x264's 4:4:4 bug only once it has seen that note, #43), `SubtitleTrack` (SRT).
- Debug launch options (Debug builds only): `PLAYER_OPEN_ITEM=<Jellyfin item id>` opens that item straight away (`PLAYER_OPEN_AT=<seconds>` starts there, e.g. near the end for Up Next); `PLAYER_DOWNLOAD_ITEM=<id>` starts downloading it; `PLAYER_IMPORT_DICTIONARIES="<zip>|…"` imports dictionaries (on Mac the zips must be inside the app's container, e.g. `~/Library/Containers/com.amatsubame.miru/Data/tmp/`); `MPV_SMOKE=1` with `MPV_SMOKE_URL`/`MPV_SMOKE_AUTH` plays one stream (`MPV_CLIP=1` tests the clipper; `REAL_CLIP_URL`/`REAL_CLIP_AUTH`/`REAL_CLIP_AT` run `RealClipTests` in `Packages/Player` against a real stream); `MPV_LOG=v` raises mpv's log level. UI tests: `UITEST_PLAYER=1`, plus `UITEST_TRANSCRIPT=1` for a saved transcript; with the local test video, `UITEST_PLAYER_START=<s>` opens it part-way in and `UITEST_PLAYER_FOLLOWING=<path>` gives it a next episode (both for the first video only).
- Live checks write to your Jellyfin: record the item's UserData first and restore it after (`POST /UserItems/{id}/UserData`). Close player windows (stop report) before restoring, or a lingering session writes its last position back when it times out. Another device can be faked with a script posting `/Sessions/Playing` + `/Progress` under a dev token (it can't receive remote Pause: Jellyfin only reports `SupportsRemoteControl` for clients with a websocket).
- Playback reports (`PlaybackModel.report`):
  - **What's sent:** a report every 10 s while playing or paused, with Pause/Unpause/TimeUpdate event names, AudioStreamIndex/SubtitleStreamIndex and `reportedAt`, plus a report right after a seek. Only time updates coalesce in the outbox.
  - **Why so often:** Jellyfin ends a session it hasn't heard from in a few minutes, and Playback Reporting only processes a progress report 20 s after the last one it used.
  - **Leaving the screen:** while playing, audio continues in the background (`UIBackgroundModes: audio`, mpv video off via `setVideoEnabled`) and so does the session. While paused (or paused from AirPods or the Lock Screen while away), the session ends with a stop; play from those controls starts a new one. Quitting on Mac ends it.
  - **Coming back:** the session restarts. If the episode was watched further elsewhere meanwhile, the player moves there, still paused, with a note.
  - **Late reports:** the handler drops starts and progress older than 2 minutes, and sends an old stop only if Jellyfin has nothing newer.
- Up Next:
  - **Next episode:** the player knows it from `session.following`, or else from the series' episode list (`JellyfinUpNext.episodes(after:)`).
  - **The card:** it shows when the credits segment starts, or 20 s before the end, and plays after a 10 s countdown. Pausing holds the countdown; Cancel hides the card for that episode. When the countdown runs out it starts `playNext` from a new task, because stopping the countdown cancels its own task.
  - **Last episode or a movie:** the end card offers `JellyfinUpNext.suggestions` from `/Items/{id}/Similar`, from the same library first and never hidden libraries. Tapping one opens it in the Watch tab (`PlayerEnvironment.openSeries`).
- **iPad and Mac sidebar:** the shown Jellyfin libraries are listed under the tabs (`SidebarLibraries` in AppCommon, as in Yomu), from the list the Watch tab last loaded (`PlayerEnvironment.libraries`). Each opens its whole library in the Watch tab, through `openSeries`.
- Now Playing (`Miru/Playback/NowPlaying.swift`):
  - **Commands:** AirPods press plays or pauses, double-press goes to the next line, triple-press goes back to the line before the one on screen (`SubtitleTrack.lineBefore`; swipes keep the music-player rule of `previous(before:)`); the Lock Screen and Control Center scrubber seeks; the Mac media key works too.
  - **Audio session:** set before mpv loads (setting it later disturbed playback). It must never mix with others: a mixing app doesn't get the AirPods, Lock Screen or Control Center controls.
    - **mpv:** its iOS audio output (`ao_audiounit`) sets the session itself each time audio starts, with mix-with-others unless `audio-exclusive=yes`. `MPVOptions.audioSession` sets that on iOS only (on the Mac it would take over the audio device).
    - **Word audio** (`Lookup/AudioService`) leaves a `.playback` session as it is.
    - **Test:** Debug builds add "mixing" to `player.video`'s value when the session mixes, and `testBackgroundAudioAndNowPlaying` checks for it.
  - **Diagnostic log:** `Application Support/nowplaying.log` in Miru's container records the session, the info set and the commands received. Read it with `devicectl … --domain-type appDataContainer --domain-identifier com.amatsubame.miru`. The simulator's Control Center has no Now Playing module.
  - **Test:** `testBackgroundAudioAndNowPlaying` needs `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_PLAYER_AUDIO_VIDEO`, a test video with an audio track.
  - **Threads:** handlers and artwork may run off the main thread.
  - **Quitting on Mac:** the stop is queued and sent at the next launch, so a live check should close the player window (Escape) before quitting, or relaunch afterwards.
- Jellyfin `/Sessions` already advances a playing session's PositionTicks between client reports (whole seconds, lagging by up to 1 s), so never add the time since `LastPlaybackCheckIn` on top. `LiveClock` keeps the best reading.
- Intro/credits skipping uses `GET /MediaSegments/{itemId}`, cached in the `mediaSegment` table. UI tests fake segments with `UITEST_PLAYER_SEGMENTS="Intro:0:30"` on the local test video.
- Kaku subtitles (milestone 6) for episodes Jimaku has nothing for: Settings ▸ Watching ▸ Subtitles ▸ Kaku (its own sign-in, `AudioServers.kakuClient`), then the Subtitles sheet's **Transcribe with Kaku** (or **the Rest of the Season**: the later episodes of the same season, in order) calls `POST /jellyfin/jobs` ([kaku.md](kaku.md)). The sheet shows the queue place or progress (polled every 5 s). The SubRip is saved as the episode's `localSubtitle` with `source: "kaku"` ("From Kaku"), so lookup, Auto-Sync, Save to Jellyfin… and offline work as for a Jimaku file; a Jimaku pick replaces it. Opening an episode with no Japanese subtitles on Jellyfin or the device checks Kaku and uses a finished transcript. Kaku transcribes a 24-minute episode in about 30 s on a mid-range consumer GPU.
- The screen stays on while a video plays (`ScreenAwake`): mpv doesn't keep it awake like Apple's player does.
- Jimaku subtitles (`Packages/JimakuKit`):
  - The API key is in the Keychain (`SecretKey.jimakuAPIKey`, Settings → Servers → Jimaku); `JIMAKU_API_KEY` in `.env` is for live checks.
  - Shows are found by AniList id, mapped from the season's AniDB id (when the library provides one, e.g. through Shokofin) through arm.haglund.dev; otherwise by name.
  - The picked file is stored per item in `localSubtitle` (it wins over Jellyfin's tracks), with the user's timing offset.
  - Nothing goes to Jellyfin by itself: "Save to Jellyfin…" in the timing panel (confirmed first) queues a `jellyfinSubtitle` outbox job (`POST /Videos/{id}/Subtitles`, base64, current timing). `LocalSubtitle.serverState` follows `editedAt`/`queuedAt`/`uploadedAt`.
  - Uploads send `Language: "ja"` (two-letter code): Jellyfin builds the file name from it, `<video name>.<language>[.forced][.sdh].<format>` (it never overwrites, adding `.0` instead). To remove a test upload: `DELETE /Videos/{id}/Subtitles/{index}`.
  - Auto-Sync (`PlayerKit/SubtitleSync`, alass's method in Swift): to the video's embedded text tracks first (`JellyfinSubtitlePicker.syncReferences`), then the downloaded English file, then the speech in the audio (`MediaClipper.speech` with libfvad from `Vendor/libfvad`, from the download or `JellyfinClient.audioOnlyStreamURL`, ~5 MB). Split penalty 20 and detector mode 1 were tuned on real episodes. The synced file replaces `data`; `originalData` keeps the download for Reset.
  - UI tests use `UITEST_JIMAKU=1` for canned answers and `UITEST_SYNC_REFERENCE=1` for a sync reference. In `Packages/Player`:
    - `REAL_SUBTITLE_FILES="<path>|…" swift test` parses real files;
    - `REAL_SYNC_PAIRS="<sub>><reference>|…"` syncs files to subtitle references;
    - `REAL_SYNC_AUDIO=<file or URL>` (with `REAL_SYNC_AUDIO_AUTH`) and `REAL_SYNC_AUDIO_SUBTITLES` sync to the audio. `REAL_SYNC_VAD_MODES` and `REAL_SYNC_PENALTIES` compare settings. Use `-c release`.
- Scrub previews: `ScrubThumbnails` uses Jellyfin trickplay if the item has it (only when the server is set to make it), else `MediaClipper.frame` from the download or the stream.
- Gesture UI test: generate a local video (any H.264 .mp4) and pass `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_PLAYER_VIDEO=<path>` (Debug builds open a local path as the item). Rotation checks: `MPV_SMOKE=1` with `MPV_SMOKE_URL=<file>`, optional `MPV_SMOKE_PAUSED=1` and `MPV_LOG=v`; count mpv's "Window size" lines after Device → Rotate.
- Keyboard shortcuts: `Miru/Playback/PlayerShortcuts.swift` is the one list (keys, titles, sections) behind the player's key handler, the Playback menu and the cheat sheet (Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts on Mac and iPad, and `?` over the video). Most are mpv's; ↑/↓ stay previous/next line.
  - **Actions:** `PlaybackModel.perform(_:)`, with an mpv-style message top left (`osd`).
  - **Matching** (`PlayerShortcut.matching`, unit-tested in `PlayerShortcutTests`): Shift is ignored for typed characters, and iOS can report Shift-[ as "[" with Shift held, so that's mapped to "{". Backspace is DEL on the Mac and BS on iOS.
  - **Menu shortcuts:** on the Mac only. On iOS, menu key commands matched Shift-[ as [ some of the time.
  - **Esc and Backspace on iOS:** they reach the player only through hidden key-command buttons.
  - **Tests:** the simulator's `typeKey` drops Shift now and then and never sends Esc or Backspace, so `PlayerShortcutsUITests` avoids those. They were checked on the Mac by posting real key events to the app's pid (`CGEvent.postToPid`).
- mpv's built-in Lua scripts must all stay off (`MPVOptions.noScripts`): the Mac refuses LuaJIT's code. mpv 0.41's `context_menu` was missing and crashed Miru on the Mac. `MPVOptionsTests.everyBuiltInScriptIsKnown` reads the scripts inside libmpv and fails on a new one.
- **Lines history grouping:** episodes of one show watched back to back are one row (`LinesHistoryModel.runs`), opening to them in history order; a single episode stays a plain row, so Lines still reads as a watch history.
- **Karaoke build-ups:** a line that the next one starts with, straight after it (「恋」 then 「恋をしたから」), is folded into the finished line when Lines are saved (`TranscriptLines.mergingBuildUps`; Core `v28_merge_build_ups` fixed saved ones). On screen the build-up still plays as drawn.
- Recently Added shows titles under the posters.
- **Player layouts** (picked from the screen's shape, e.g. on the iPhone Duo): `PlayerLayout` (overlay, controls in the black bars, or the picture over `PlayerLinesStrip`), placed in screen coordinates by `PlaybackView.place`. The Mac always uses the overlay. Tests: `PlayerLayoutTests`.
