# Shared setup, dictionaries, mining and downloads

Notes that apply to more than one app.

- **Data:** each app has its own container. The **shared setup** (App Group `group.com.amatsubame`, `Core/SharedSetup` + `SetupSharing`) holds dictionaries (one copy of the files), audio sources, AnkiConnect (account, settings, key in the shared Keychain group), mined-word marks, lookup counts and the popup's settings (`lookupCount`, `popupSettings`: Settings ▸ Lookup and Mining ▸ Popup), and the Hachidori host the Mac apps link to (`hachidoriSettings`: Settings ▸ Lookup and Mining ▸ Hachidori; docs/hachidori.md).
  - Each app's Settings has "Share Setup with My Other Apps" (on by default): a new app fills in from it, the first set-up app publishes its own, and an app with both asks. Turning it off copies the setup back; switching applies on the next launch.
  - Servers, progress, downloads, outbox and lookup history stay per app. Hiku and its extension share their own folder in the group, with the choice kept in the group's defaults (`setupSharing.lookup`).
  - On devices and the Mac the App Group needs a team in the paid Apple Developer Program (a free personal team can't sign it). Simulator builds always have it (`Shared/SharedSetup.entitlements`); elsewhere it's on when `Config/Signing.local.xcconfig` sets `AMATSUBAME_APP_GROUP = YES` (device: `Shared/SharedSetup.entitlements`; Mac: the `-AppGroup` variants of `Shared/App-macOS.entitlements` and `HikuExtension/HikuExtension-macOS.entitlements`). Without it everything runs independently. `group.com.amatsubame` is registered to the project owner's team; another team needs its own group ID (`SharedSetup.groupIdentifier` and the entitlements). The Keychain items use the App Group as their access group, so no separate Keychain group is needed.

- **Setting up a simulator for hands-on testing** (`DevSetup`, AppCommon, Debug builds only): launch an app once with `xcrun simctl launch` and `SIMCTL_CHILD_DEV_SETUP=1` plus `DEV_…` values, and it signs in through the same models as Settings and logs each step to `Application Support/dev-setup.log`. The first app sets up what's shared: `DEV_ANKI_URL/KEY`, `DEV_ANKI_SETTINGS` (AnkiSettings JSON, e.g. a scratch deck and the tag `amatsubame-test`, so test cards are easy to find and delete), `DEV_AUDIO_SOURCES` (AudioSourceRecord JSON), `DEV_IMPORT_DICTIONARIES` (zips in order); the others fill in from the shared setup. Per app: `DEV_KAVITA_URL/KEY` (Yomu), `DEV_JELLYFIN_URL/USER/PASSWORD` (Miru), `DEV_ABS_URL/KEY` (Yomu, Kiku), `DEV_KAKU_URL/TOKEN`, `DEV_HIDDEN_LIBRARIES` (ids, `|`-separated), `DEV_KECHIMOCHI_URL` (each app's own). `DEV_HOLD_PROGRESS=1` leaves a marker so that simulator never sends reading, watching or listening progress (it stays queued). Hiku's extension: `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_ENABLE_EXTENSION_ONLY=1 xcodebuild test -scheme Hiku -only-testing:HikuUITests/ExtensionLookupUITests/testEnableExtensionOnly` turns it on in Settings (then uninstall the runner). Manga OCR: copy a `compiled/` models folder into Yomu's `Application Support/OCRModels`.
- Real dictionaries go in the gitignored `Dictionaries/` folder (for example Jitendex, JPDB frequency and Kanjium). Use them with:
  - Package tests: `REAL_DICTIONARY_ZIPS="<zip>|<zip>|…" swift test --filter RealDictionaryTests` in `Packages/Dictionary`
  - Popup renders (macOS): `REAL_DICTIONARY_ZIPS="<zip>|…" POPUP_RENDER_DIR=<folder> swift test --filter RealPopupRenderTests` in `Packages/Lookup` writes the popup for a few sample words as HTML and dark-mode PNGs, to check its layout by eye
  - UI tests: `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_DICTIONARY_ZIPS="<zip>|…" xcodebuild test … -only-testing:YomuUITests/DictionaryUITests` (without it they use a tiny generated dictionary)
- Mining UI tests (Yomu): `testMiningAWordFromAPage` runs with the OCR env (cards stay queued). To send real cards, add `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_ANKI_URL` and `TEST_RUNNER_UITEST_ANKI_DECK=<scratch deck>` (create it with `createDeck`; `testCardMinedOfflineIsSentAfterReconnecting` also needs both). Afterwards delete the scratch notes, their `amatsubame_*` media files and the deck. Never point tests at a real deck.
- AnkiConnect compatibility: some AnkiConnect proxies are stricter than AnkiConnect itself, so the apps stick to a safe subset: no extra params; media goes up with `storeMediaFile` before `addNote` (some proxies silently drop `addNote`'s `picture`/`audio`), and media names use only `[a-z0-9_.]` (`AnkiNoteHandler.safeFilename`), since some lowercase file names; nothing relies on `getMediaFilesNames`, `apiReflect` or `guiBrowse`, or on `storeMediaFile`'s return value.
- Word marks: `MinedWordStore` keeps every note of the mining deck (Settings ▸ Cards) with `known` = a card in review with an interval of 21 days or more, not being relearned (`deck:"…" is:review -is:learn prop:ivl>=21`, Hachidori's test); queued cards count as learning. Words marked known or ignored by hand (`WordStatusStore`, the popup's Known and Ignore, Settings ▸ Anki ▸ Known and Ignored Words; shared through the setup and with Hachidori, docs/hachidori.md section 8) are never marked: `MiningService.markedByHand` goes into `WordMarks`. `WordMarks.state` lets a word with no note of its own take a kana spelling's state (Hachidori's rule: 可愛い from a かわいい note, and back through the note's reading). `MiningService.refreshMinedWords` runs at launch and every 30 minutes, and `revision` bumps when states change. `WordScanner` (Dictionary) splits text into words on its own hoshidicts lookup (longest deinflected match, starting only at kanji, katakana or a hiragana prefix before kanji; marks words with kanji and katakana words of 2+). Miru: `WordMarkCache` (Lookup) per line, drawn by `TappableLine`. Yomu books: `BookReader.js` `wordRuns`/`markWords` wrap words in `<km-w>` (its own element name, so XPath positions don't move), split per section and cached as `.words-v1-<section>.json` in the book folder. Switch: Settings ▸ Lookup ▸ Mark Words by Anki State (`AnkiSettings.marksWords`).
- First meaning (`Lookup/CompactDefinitionSummary.swift`): a line-for-line port of Hachidori's compact definition summary (render/popup.js), drawn beside each headword by `EntryRenderer.compactSummary` when `PopupSettings.showCompactDefinitionSummary` is on (off by default, as in Hachidori). `RealCompactSummaryTests` dumps real lookups with our summaries (`COMPACT_SUMMARY_DUMP`) so they can be compared with Hachidori's own functions run in Node; at the port, 357 of 357 matched.
- Popup background opacity (`PopupSettings.backgroundOpacityPercent`, Hachidori's Background opacity, 85 % by default): `LookupPopupView` draws the popup colour at that opacity behind itself (`PopupBackground`) and clears the sheet's or popover's own background, while its page stays transparent (`document(for:nativeBackground: true)`). A wrapper with its own header (Kiku's clip bar, the book reader's header and Duo panel) passes `drawsBackground: false` and applies `lookupPopupBackground` around everything. Pages without a native popup (Hiku's extension) draw it in CSS (`--popup-opacity` on `html`).
- Popup glass: what's behind is always blurred at full strength (`PopupBackground` puts `.ultraThinMaterial` behind the tint; Hiku's frame host has `backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(1.5) brightness(0.72)`, `1.08` in light mode). Background Opacity is only the tint, as in Hachidori. (A Background Blur setting faded the material, which cross-fades a sharp and a blurred copy of what's behind; it was dropped, migration `v37_no_popup_blur`.) Text: white on dark (secondary `#DADAE0`), black on light (`#3A3A40`); tags, chips and example cards are see-through with a 1px edge (`--chip-edge`); a text halo (`--popup-halo` = `LookupService.haloStrength`, 1 at 0 % opacity, 0 from 70 %) is the page's `text-shadow` and `PopupTextHalo` on native bars.
- Lookup counts (`Core/LookupCounts.swift`, `LookupService.record`): Hachidori's lookup statistics (docs/hachidori.md section 4). +1 for the first result's headword and reading (trimmed, NFC) per visit: `lookup(text:)`, a link in a definition (`LookupPopupView` `.query`) and a settled search (`recordSearch`); Back and searching as you type don't count. The chip (`EntryRenderer.LookupCount`) follows the first result's frequencies in Hachidori's wording; a count recorded after the page is shown (a search) is filled in by `LookupWebView` without a reload.
- **Refresh on the Mac:** View ▸ Refresh (⌘R, `RefreshCommand` in each app's commands; an iPad keyboard gets it too) posts `.refreshRequested`, and screens that pull to refresh load again: `refreshableByCommand` is `refreshable` plus that (Miru's Watch, series and Lines; Kiku's Listen, library, show, book and Marks; Yomu's library, grid and series page listen for it themselves).
- **The lookup's bottom panel** (`LookupOverlayHost`, narrow screens): its handle's drag is measured in global coordinates. Measured in the handle's own, which move as the panel resizes, the drag read as jitter: the panel rubber-banded, and a pull down could end as a flick up to full height. `LookupOverlayPlacement.panelDrag` decides the outcome from the finger's real direction (speed only helps a short flick); `KikuLookupPanelUITests` pulls it slowly.
- **Look Up on Hover (the Mac):** `TappableLine` (Miru's subtitles and lines, Kiku's transcript) looks up the character under the pointer as a click would, when `PopupSettings.hoverLookup` allows it: a key held (Shift by default; `HoverModifiers` follows the keys with a local event monitor, so pressing it over a word counts), no key at all (`always`, after resting 180 ms), or never (`off`). It skips characters inside the word already showing. It's the Mac's own setting, not one of Hachidori's options, so Settings ▸ Popup keeps it changeable while linked. Yomu's books do the same in the page (`TextTapBridge.js`'s `setHover`, switched on by `BookWebController` as each section loads; a hover never reports a miss, which would turn the page; `HoverBridgeTests` runs it in a web view). Manga pages do it on their OCR blocks (docs/yomu.md); `HoverModifiers` lives in the Lookup package so the Readers package can share it.
- **Close When the Pointer Leaves (the Mac):** `PopupSettings.closeOnPointerExit` (off by default, the Mac's own like the hover key). `LookupOverlayHost` watches the card with `onHover`: once the pointer has been inside, leaving closes the popup after 250 ms unless it comes back or a sheet is up. Every app's popup goes through `lookupOverlay`, so it applies to all of them.
- Stats tab: `ActivityLog` (Core/Stats.swift) keeps `activitySession` rows in each app's own database: reading adds characters (books, only real page moves, capped at 2,500 a move) or pages (manga, one page or spread forward), Miru adds seconds that played forward (no seeks); a pause over 5 minutes starts a new session. `lookupEvent` counts taps that found results. Cards mined come from the mining deck's note ids (creation times), cached in `statsState`, plus this app's unsent cards; per series from this app's outbox cards (`MinedCard.seriesKey/seriesTitle`, older ones by their MiscInfo title). The shared screen is `StatsView` (AppCommon, Swift Charts). Yomu's chart counts a `StatsSummary.Measure` the reader picks from its title (time by default, since manga has no characters and books no pages; remembered in `stats.readingMeasure`), with the other two as tiles; the Today row shows 字, pages or both, whichever the day has. `UITEST_STATS=1` seeds a week of data. Nothing is sent to kechimochi; Settings ▸ Stats holds its URL. Downloads and the cache are in Settings ▸ Storage (`StorageView` in each app).
- Downloads (phase 8): records in the `download` table (`DownloadStore`), shared UI in `AppCommon/DownloadViews.swift`. Miru's episodes go through `EpisodeDownloader` (background URLSession, files in Application Support/Downloads); Yomu's volumes through `VolumeDownloader` (pinned pages in `PageCacheStore`, pinned books in `BookRepository`, both under Application Support/ReaderStorage, not Caches). Nothing downloads unless the user asks, except Yomu's automatic downloads, which the user turns on per library (`AutoDownloadManager` in Yomu/Library/AutoDownloads.swift, rules in `KavitaKit/AutoDownloadPlanner`, settings in `autoDownloadSetting`): a started series (Kavita On Deck or read on this device) keeps the volume being read plus N after it, and finished volumes can be removed (never the open one). A manga volume is done once its pages are in; OCR follows as its own step (`Extra.textPending`). Offline (or Kavita unreachable) the Library shows only series with downloads. Use `path(percentEncoded: false)` for FileManager paths (Application Support has a space).
- Mac: Yomu reads in the main window (`AppEnvironment.readingSession` covers the tabs), and Miru plays there too (`PlayerEnvironment.playingSession`; Escape or Playback → Close Video ⌘[). Menus are `ReaderMenus` and `PlayerMenus` (focused values `readerPaging`, `readerClose`, `playback`).
- Driving the Mac app for live checks: launch with `--args -ApplePersistenceIgnoreState YES` (restored reader windows otherwise catch clicks and turn pages), and restore any reading progress a test changes.
- **Shared screens:**
  - **Cards:** replaced by the one Anki page (below).
  - **Dictionary search:** it takes romaji (`Romaji` in the Dictionary package: hiragana first, then katakana for loanwords), and a typed search counts once in Stats when submitted or left on screen for 1.5 s.
  - **Audio Sources:** URL keys (`apiKey`, `token`…) show masked and are kept in the Keychain, not the database (`Lookup/AudioSourceKeys`). The saved template has `{key:<id>}` in each key's place, and one Keychain item (`audioSource.keys`) holds every source's keys as JSON. It's in the shared group with the AnkiConnect key when the setup is shared, and is copied along when an app joins or leaves. Keys saved in the database by older builds move there at the next launch.
  - **Tabs:** tapping the tab that's showing returns it to its top screen in Yomu, Miru and Kiku. On iOS 26 that's the system's own pop to root (iPhone's tab bar and iPad's top tabs): the selection setter in each root view (`tabSelection`, which bumps the tab content's `.id`) isn't called for the tab already selected, so the bump doesn't run there; it's kept for where the setter does see the tap. `SettingsRetapUITests` (Kiku) and `testSettingsTabReturnsToTheTopFromTwoScreensDeep` (Yomu, Miru) check Settings two screens deep (`SharedUITests/SettingsRetapSupport.swift`); the tabs whose stack is driven by a path of their own are checked too: Yomu's Library (`testLibraryTabReturnsToTheTopFromTwoScreensDeep`), Miru's Lines (`testLinesTabReturnsToTheTop`, in memory) and Watch (`WatchRetapUITests`, signed in by DevSetup), Kiku's Listen (`ListenRetapUITests`, signed in by DevSetup). iOS's pop clears those paths too. In #166's run the re-tap was taken by iOS's "Save Password?" sheet (shown after the Kaku token was typed) and its closing animation, not by the app.
- **Stats across devices** (`StatsSync` in Core, `StatsCloudSync` in AppCommon): each app's activity log is copied to the app's iCloud folder so the same app on another device counts it too.
  - **Files, not a database:** `Stats/<yomu|miru|kiku>/<device>_<yyyy-MM>.json` in the ubiquity container `iCloud.com.amatsubame` (`StatsSyncFile`: that device's sessions starting in that UTC month, and its lookups). A device only writes its own files and only reads the others', so nothing is ever merged or in conflict, and there's no CloudKit schema to deploy.
  - **Rows:** `activitySession.device` and `lookupEvent.device` are nil for what this device logged and the other device's name for what came in; a changed file replaces that device's rows for that month. `ActivityLog.sessions` counts both (`localOnly:` for the two callers that must not: the one-time Audiobookshelf session send and Kiku's download rules); `record` only ever extends this device's own session.
  - **What's sent and read** is remembered in `statsSyncFile` (a hash of each file written, the date of each file read), so a pass with no news writes and reads nothing.
  - **A copied database** (a restored backup): `deviceHardware` (a hash of identifierForVendor or the Mac's hardware UUID) no longer matches, so the old log stays under the old device's name and this one starts a new name.
  - **When:** as the app comes forward (at most once a minute), as it's put away, and when Stats opens or is pulled down. Settings ▸ Stats ▸ Sync with iCloud (on by default) with the last sync or why not; the one switch covers Stats and the synced records below.
  - **Builds:** the entitlement is behind `AMATSUBAME_ICLOUD` (Config/Signing.xcconfig), which the upload and release scripts set; without it (the simulator, plain device builds) there's no iCloud folder and nothing happens. It can't be tried on a simulator: `StatsSyncTests` runs two databases against one folder.
- **Synced records** (`RecordSync` in Core, run in the same pass as Stats): small things kept the same on every device, one `SyncedCollection` per kind, chosen per app (`CommonEnvironment.syncedCollections`): Yomu's daily goals (`DailyGoalCollection`) and manga and book reader settings, Miru's player settings, Kiku's listening and per-show settings and marks with their notes (`TableCollection.audioMarks`, keyed by `audioMark.uuid`, v66; the row id and `minedJobId` stay per device, so undoing a card works only where it was mined). Clear and the day-after-mining prune are deletions like any other. Also hidden libraries (`hiddenLibraries`, `hiddenJellyfinLibraries`, `japaneseOnlyJellyfinLibraries`, Kiku's `kikuHiddenLibraries`: that one column of `listenSettings`, via `only:`), all keyed by their server (`TableCollection.serverScoped`, `SyncServerKey`); Yomu's read-along pairings (`ReadAlongPairCollection`: the choice only, keyed by Kavita chapter and server; a pairing moved to another audiobook drops the alignment, which each device makes itself); Miru's Jimaku timing (`SubtitleTimingCollection`: source, file and offset per Jellyfin item, applied only where the same file is, and a subtitle is never removed by another device); the kechimochi address (`kechimochiAddress`, `statsState.kechimochiURL` only); and, in a shared setup without a linked Hachidori, the popup's settings (`TableCollection.popupSettings`, a second pass on the setup's database as app `setup`, each app writing `setup-<app>-<device>.json`, every app reading all of them).
  - **Settings tables** (`TableCollection`, Core/SyncedSettings.swift): each row is a record, its key column the id and the other columns the value as sorted JSON (a JSON column, like the reader settings, nested so it always reads the same). `excluded` columns stay per device and are left alone when a record is applied (Miru's `maxStreamBitrate`; Kiku's `hiddenLibraries`, which go with the server later, and `watchLiveText`). A missing row is inserted with the table's defaults for those. Reader settings per series are `kavita.series.<id>@<server>`, the server a hash of the Kavita address (`SyncServerKey`), and only applied on a device with the same server; `global` (Use for All) goes everywhere. A per-show setting back to plain deletes its row, which syncs as a deletion. A reader or player already open keeps the settings it loaded until it's opened again.
  - **Files:** `Sync/<collection>/<app>-<device>.json` (`RecordSyncFile`), one per app per device, holding the records whose newest edit that device made: `{id, value (JSON text), editedAt (ms), deleted}`. The device name is Stats'. An app reads only its own app's files.
  - **Order:** each pass notes every collection's local edits first, then applies and writes each: two collections can share a row (Kiku's playback settings and its hidden libraries), and a row one creates by applying isn't the other's edit.
  - **Blank records:** before a collection's first sync on a device, a record whose synced values are all empty (NULL, an empty list or text) isn't an edit: an address never set would otherwise clear the one set elsewhere. Afterwards, clearing it is an edit.
  - **Local edits** aren't hooked: each pass compares the collection's records (`records(_:)`, id → JSON) with `syncRecord` (the last merged state) and stamps what changed or vanished. A stamp is `max(now, newest edit seen + 1 ms)`, so a device whose clock is behind still stamps after what it has seen.
  - **Merge:** another device's record is applied (`apply(id:value:)`) when it's newer than this one's; at the same time, the larger device id wins, so both sides agree. A record the collection doesn't take on this device (a series of another Kavita server) is neither applied nor remembered, since a remembered record missing from the table would read as a local deletion and go out as one. A deletion is a tombstone, kept 90 days (`tombstoneLife`) and then dropped; a device away longer than that could bring a deleted record back.
  - **Daily goals:** one record per kind and calendar day (`reading/2026-11-23`), so days merge and each keeps its goal; the "reached" note's date stays in this device's defaults.
  - **Tests:** `RecordSyncTests` (two databases, one folder): edits both ways, ties, tombstones and their expiry, a slow clock, goals by day, other apps' files ignored. `SyncedSettingsTests`: reader settings on the same server (and not another's), Miru's bitrate and Kiku's watch and libraries kept per device, a per-show setting removed; the other server's device doesn't delete a series' settings. `SyncedRestTests`: hidden libraries by server, Miru's Japanese-only libraries, Kiku's hidden libraries beside its settings, pairings (an alignment dropped with a new audiobook), Jimaku timing (same file only, never removed), the kechimochi address (a blank doesn't win; a clear does), popup settings across apps and devices. `SetupSyncHachidoriTests`: linked, the popup settings are neither sent nor taken; unlinked, they sync (`RecordSync.syncSharedSetup`). `SyncedMarksTests`: a mark, a note edited on both (the later wins), mined on one (shown mined, the job local), cleared, pruned, ids for existing marks.
- **Stats ▸ All immersion**: Stats in Yomu, Miru and Kiku switches between the app's own numbers and kechimochi's (`AllImmersionView`, `ImmersionModel`).
  - **Source:** it reads kechimochi 0.3.3 through `KechimochiKit`, which can only use `/api/version`, `/api/dashboard/snapshot` and `/api/dashboard/heatmap-year`. Those are read-only, though the last two are POSTs. kechimochi has no login, so KechimochiKit must never call `/api/reset`, imports or deletes. The address is the page set in Settings ▸ Stats.
  - **What it shows:**
    - the heatmap: weeks from Sunday, shaded by quarters of the year's days with activity, the busiest always brightest (`ImmersionHeatmap`), with a year menu;
    - the Total, Longest streak and Daily average tiles;
    - time by activity type in each app's color (Listening Kiku, Watching Miru, Reading Yomu, others grey).
  - **Offline:** the last answers are kept (Core `v29_kechimochi_cache`), so it shows them with their age.
  - **Never combined:** kechimochi's totals are never added to the app's own; the footer says so.
- **iPhone Duo** (the foldable): `Fold.swift` in Lookup, `FoldLayout.swift` in AppCommon.
  - **`FoldReader`** (in Lookup, so the readers can use it) gives a view the fold crossing it (`Fold`, also `Fold.crossing(geometry)`, and `onHingeChange`): the crease's frame with its margins, from iOS 27.1's `reservedRegions(kind: .division)`, and the hinge from `onHingeChange`. In the simulator the crease is reported on the inner screen only, 41 pt wide: down the middle in landscape (x 455–496 of 951), across in portrait (y 455–496 of 951). The system marks it inactive while the screen lies fully open; `Fold.isActive` says so, but layouts avoid it either way. The outer screen reports no hinge in the simulator, so Yomu treats a phone that has reported one, with no crease crossing it, as folded.
  - **Side safe areas:** the outer screen's clock and status column (and the tab bar beside it) are a side safe area. Horizontal shelves use `shelfScrollClip()` so they stop there instead of `scrollClipDisabled()` running under it, and manga pages stay out of it.
  - **Side by side** (`WideSplitStack`: Miru's Lines, Kiku's Listen): on a wide screen (`WideColumns`: at the fold, or 700+ points wide) the first screen is on the left and what it opens on the right. The left stack's path always reads empty and hands a route to the right. A `NavigationLink` there still made the left stack start a push, so the list slid away and back on every pick. `SplitLink` (every link on Lines' and Listen's first screens) opens the route on the right directly when side by side (`openInWideSplit`) and is a `NavigationLink` otherwise; `rowStyle` makes a list row take the tap across its width, otherwise the caller's button style applies (Kiku's bordered See All, its book cards). Debug builds count the left stack's attempted pushes (`split.leftPushes`, for `testWideLinesListStaysPutWhenPickingAnEpisode` and Kiku's `testWideListenStaysPutWhenOpeningLibrary`, which needs DevSetup).

- **Settings, setup and recommended dictionaries:**
  - **Settings:** every app is built from `SettingsKit` (AppCommon) in one order:
    - `SettingsStatusCard`: a chip per server checked read-only when Settings opens; servers not set up are greyed. It also shows what's waiting to sync (`OutboxStore.waitingCounts`) and startup errors.
    - The app's own section.
    - `LookupAndMiningSection`, whose Anki row opens `AnkiSettingsPage`: the connection, cards and AnkiWeb on one page, with Miru's Card Picture in its extras slot.
    - `StatsSettingsLink`.
    - `SettingsFooterSection`: Storage, Advanced and About.
  - **Advanced** (`AdvancedSettingsView`): Share Setup, Waiting to Sync by kind (`OutboxView(kind:)`, which can clear progress kinds; a job waiting for another one, a handler's `OutboxWait`, shows as "Waiting" with its reason, never as a retry or "Needs attention": it's looked at again every 30 s with no attempt counted (`OutboxStore.markWaiting`, the `waitingFor` column, v60), so a long wait never grows its back-off), Run Setup Again, and Troubleshooting with Export Logs.
  - **Logs:** `AppLog` (Core) keeps a rolling `Application Support/Logs/app.log` of server errors, sync, dictionaries and setup, blanking tokens, keys and passwords. Export Logs shares it with the debug logs.
  - **First-launch setup** (`SetupFlow`, `SetupSteps`, AppCommon):
    - **Steps:** each app supplies its server, libraries and extras steps; `SetupProgress` keeps the step per app.
    - **Existing installs:** setup is marked finished once, while it hasn't started and the server is connected. Run Setup Again (`setup.runAgain()`) shows it at once.
    - **Shared setup:** `SharedSetup.origin` (`origin.json`) records which app made it, for "Use your existing setup?". `SharedSetup.members` (`members.json`) records each app's choice when it opens or changes it, so "Shared with …" lists only the apps that share. "Set Up Separately" switches the app to its own setup from the next launch (setup says how to close and reopen it), resuming at Dictionaries.
    - **Tests:** `DevSetup` and the in-memory UI test modes skip setup.
  - **Frequency sort:** `DictionaryLibrary.reloadEngine` picks the sort dictionary (Settings' choice, else the first enabled with frequencies) and its direction (`FrequencyOrder`, from its `index.json` `frequencyMode`: "occurrence-based" descending, rank-based or unset ascending, as Yomitan's `sortFrequencyDictionaryOrder` and Hachidori's `frequencyModeOrder`), and gives both to `DictionaryEngine` and `WordScanner`. hoshidicts' AUTO means no sort dictionary at all (lookup.cpp's `LookupFrequencyOrder::Auto`), so it's never sent. While linked, Hachidori's `frequencyOrder` (ascending or descending) wins (`LookupService.keep` → `setHostFrequencyOrder`); "auto" or "disabled" there leaves the dictionary's own. `FrequencyOrderTests`.
  - **Character names** (`Packages/Names`, `CharacterNameService`, one per app in `CommonEnvironment.characterNames`): a Yomitan dictionary titled "Character Names" (`NameRanking.characterNamesTitle`) holding the casts of the series each app has open (`characterNameOpenSeries`, one row per app: at most Yomu's book and Miru's show), installed into the shared library just above JMnedict (`placeAboveNames`), rebuilt on every open and close, removed when nothing is open or the switch (`characterNameSettings`) is off. Same casts again: same revision (a hash), not reinstalled.
    - **Which AniList series** (`CharacterNameMatch`, kept per series key `kavita|jellyfin:<server hash>:<id>`): the server's AniList id (Kavita's `aniListId`, 0 = none; checked against the test Kavita: 13 of 30 series had one), else its MAL id (`Media(idMal:)`), else MangaBaka's `/v2/series/match` when it names one AniList id, else AniList's search when exactly one result has the same title (letters and digits compared) and fits the kind (a light novel's NOVEL, a manga's not). Anything less sure is stored with no media and the series page offers **Pick the Series** (`CharacterNamePicker`, AniList search; "None of These" says it has none). Nothing is guessed.
    - **Casts** (`characterCast`): AniList's MAIN and SUPPORTING characters (`characters(sort: [ROLE, RELEVANCE, ID])`, 25 a page, at most 8 pages; BACKGROUND and descriptions left out), kept 30 days and used stale when AniList can't be reached; the newest 50 kept. `RequestLimiter` spaces AniList requests 2 s apart (its limit is 30 a minute at the moment) and waits out a 429's `Retry-After` (a minute without one). Portraits (`PortraitStore`, `Character Portraits/` beside the dictionaries): re-encoded to ≤150 px JPEGs, about 7 KB.
    - **Spellings and readings** (`NameForms`, `NameRomaji`): the whole name (and with its ・), family and given when the split is sure (a space, or both halves confirmed by JMnedict or as kana spelling the romaji: 夢見|ひかり), Japanese aliases; no honorific forms. A reading is kana's own, else JMnedict's reading that spells AniList's romaji (`NameRomaji.key`: Hepburn, long vowels as ou/uu, m before b/p/m as n), else the romaji's kana unless it's unsure (ō, "oh"): then none. Descriptive "names" (王様, …の…) are left out.
    - **Ranking:** names sort as a name dictionary in `NameRanking` (a kana-converted match goes below words). With Hachidori linked, the Mac's lookups go to the host, which doesn't have these: `LookupService.rawTerms` merges the local names' hits after every host result as long or longer (`NameRanking.merging`).
    - **Yomu:** `ReaderContainer` opens the series (`AppEnvironment.namedSeries`) and closes it on disappear; the series page has More ▸ Character Names… and the pick row. Miru comes in the next PR (Jellyfin provider ids, AniDB by search plus a strict check).
    - **Tests:** `NamesTests` (made-up casts: readings, splits, the builder installed into a real library and looked up, matching, caching, closing, the rate limit), `HostLookupTests.linkedLookupsKeepTheLocalCharacterNames`, KavitaKit's ids, and `CharacterNamesUITests` (live test Kavita with AniList faked by `UITEST_NAMES_FIXTURES`: pick, the cast ready in the reader, gone after closing).
  - **Recommended dictionaries:** `RecommendedDictionaries` is the catalog: Hachidori's defaults (Jitendex, JMnedict, Bee's Ultimate Kanji, Jiten global frequency, Bee's Ultimate Grammar; `defaultSelection`) and Kanjium, unticked (`selectedByDefault: false`); KANJIDIC is `retired` (still updated and credited where installed, not offered); `DictionaryUpdater` installs it and checks weekly.
    - **Source table:** `dictionarySource` (Core `v31`) records each dictionary's catalog id and its own `index.json` update address. It's part of the shared setup.
    - **Updates:** an update keeps the dictionary's id, order and on/off, installing into a new folder (`DictionaryRecord.folder`) beside the old one; the frequency sort follows the new title. A revision the index names but a download doesn't bring is kept as `undeliveredRevision` and not tried again.
    - **When updates run:** automatic checks run only on Wi-Fi outside Low Data Mode, one app at a time (`.update-lock`). Setup and Add Recommended download on cellular too.
    - **Deleted dictionaries:** deleting a recommended one is remembered (`removedRecommended`).
    - **Hiku:** its own setup is never updated (the extension's built-in dictionaries).
  - **Testing setup:**
    - `UITEST_SETUP_DATA=<name>` gives a launch its own data set: folder, Keychain items, defaults and shared setup. Two apps given the same name share a setup.
    - `SetupUITests` in YomuUITests, MiruUITests and KikuUITests read test accounts on your servers from `.env` (`TEST_*`, `SharedUITests/SetupUITestSupport.swift`) and skip without them. Use dedicated non-admin accounts, not your own. `TEST_KAVITA_LIBRARIES`, `TEST_JELLYFIN_LIBRARIES` and `TEST_ABS_LIBRARIES` name two of each account's libraries as `<Japanese-named library>|<English-named library>` (e.g. `漫画|Books`), to check setup's library guess. Miru's and Kiku's tests need Yomu installed; they make a shared setup with Yomu's DevSetup.
    - `UITEST_STALE_DICTIONARY=<catalog id>` makes an installed one look out of date, for Check Now.
